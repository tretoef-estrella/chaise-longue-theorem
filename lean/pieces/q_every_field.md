# Equality over every field (Theorem 5.9′, the field part), and Theorem B with equality

This file uses, unchanged: `q_peeling_lemma.md` (`C_m`), `q_P1_fibres.md` (the setting `T` with a fixed-point-free involution, `λ(M)`, `Z_Λ`), `q_P3_identities.md` (tight patterns, their products, `V_Λ`), `q_P3_lifts.md` (`Par_m`, down-sets of `Par_m`), `q_induction.md` (its Theorem, `dim V_Λ ≥ |Z_Λ|` over every field), `q_theorem_B_lower.md` (`(D_J : J ∈ 𝒥)`, `Q_k(q)`, parts (i) and (iii)) and `q_support.md` (its Theorem, equality when `F` contains `q − 1` distinct `(q−1)`-th roots of unity).

## Setting

Let `q ≥ 3` be odd and `h := (q − 1)/2`. For a field `F`, `C_m(F) := F[y_1, …, y_m]/(y_1^{q−1}, …, y_m^{q−1})` has the `F`-basis of box monomials `y^a`, `a ∈ B_m := {0, …, q−2}^m`. `V_Λ(F) ⊆ C_m(F)` is the ideal generated by the tight-pattern products of the elements of `Λ` (on the indices `1, …, m`).

For `Z_Λ` take any finite set `T` with a fixed-point-free involution and `|T| = 2h` (the Setting of `q_P1_fibres.md`); `|Z_Λ|` does not depend on the choice (part (0) below).

## Theorem

**(0)** If `T`, `T'` are two such sets, there is a bijection `σ : T → T'` with `σ(−u) = −σ(u)`, and then `λ(σ ∘ M) = λ(M)` for every `M ∈ T^m`; hence `|Z_Λ(T)| = |Z_Λ(T')|`.

**(i) (integral spanning set)** Every tight-pattern product `G` is the image of a polynomial `G_ℤ ∈ ℤ[y_1, …, y_m]` that does not depend on `F` (the same formula: `D(y_a, y_b) = Σ_{i=0}^{q−2} (−1)^i y_a^i y_b^{q−2−i}` and `Δ(B)` have integer coefficients). As an `F`-subspace, `V_Λ(F)` is spanned by the images of the finitely many integer vectors `v_{a,G} ∈ ℤ^{B_m}` (the coefficients of `y^a·G_ℤ` reduced modulo `(y_i^{q−1})`), for `a ∈ B_m` and `G` a tight-pattern product of an element of `Λ`. Hence `dim_F V_Λ(F) = rank_F(A)`, where `A` is the integer matrix with rows `v_{a,G}` and `rank_F(A)` is the rank of its image over `F`.

**(ii) (rank under a ring map from ℤ)** For an integer matrix `A` and a field `F`: `rank_F(A) ≤ rank_ℚ(A)`, with equality when `char F = 0`.

**(iii) (characteristic 0)** If `char F = 0`, then `dim_F V_Λ(F) = |Z_Λ|` for every `m` and every down-set `Λ` of `Par_m`.

**(iv) (every field)** For every field `F`, every `m ≥ 0` and every down-set `Λ` of `Par_m`: `dim_F V_Λ(F) = |Z_Λ|`.

**(v) (Theorem B with equality)** For every field `F`, every odd `q ≥ 3` and every `k ≥ 0`: `dim_F (D_J : J ∈ 𝒥) = Q_k(q)`.

## Proofs

(0) Choose one element in each orbit `{u, −u}` of `T` and of `T'`; both have `h` orbits, so match the orbits by any bijection and send `u ↦ u'`, `−u ↦ −u'`. The counts `a_κ`, `ā_κ` of `σ ∘ M` are those of `M` on the corresponding class (possibly with `u` and `−u` exchanged, which does not change `|a_κ − ā_κ|`), so `λ(σ ∘ M) = λ(M)`, and `M ↦ σ ∘ M` is a bijection `Z_Λ(T) → Z_Λ(T')`.

(i) `V_Λ(F)` is the ideal generated by the products `G`, so as an `F`-space it is spanned by `y^a·G`, `a ∈ B_m` (the box monomials span `C_m(F)`). The map `ℤ[y] → F[y]` is a ring map sending `G_ℤ ↦ G` and the ideal `(y_i^{q−1})` into `(y_i^{q−1})`, so the coordinates of `y^a·G` in the monomial basis are the images of the integer coordinates of `y^a·G_ℤ` modulo `(y_i^{q−1})`.

(ii) The rank of a matrix over a field is the largest `r` such that some `r × r` minor is non-zero. Every minor of the image of `A` over `F` is the image of the corresponding integer minor. If it is non-zero in `F`, the integer minor is non-zero, hence non-zero in `ℚ`: so `rank_F(A) ≤ rank_ℚ(A)`. If `char F = 0`, then `ℤ → F` is injective and a minor is non-zero in `F` iff it is non-zero in `ℤ` iff it is non-zero in `ℚ`: equality.

(iii) Let `K := ℂ` (or any field of characteristic `0` containing a primitive `(q−1)`-th root of unity `ζ`). The set `T_K := {ζ^j : 0 ≤ j < q − 1}` has `q − 1` elements and `Π_{u∈T_K}(X − u) = X^{q−1} − 1`, so the Theorem of `q_support.md` gives `dim_K V_Λ(K) = |Z_Λ(T_K)|`. By (i) and (ii), `dim_F V_Λ(F) = rank_F(A) = rank_ℚ(A) = rank_K(A) = dim_K V_Λ(K)`, and `|Z_Λ(T_K)| = |Z_Λ|` by (0).

(iv) By (i) and (ii), and (iii) for `ℚ`: `dim_F V_Λ(F) = rank_F(A) ≤ rank_ℚ(A) = dim_ℚ V_Λ(ℚ) = |Z_Λ|`. The reverse inequality is the Theorem of `q_induction.md` (valid over every field). ∎

(v) By part (i) of `q_theorem_B_lower.md`, `(D_J : J ∈ 𝒥) = V_{(1)}` with `m = 2k + 1` and `{(1)}` a down-set of `Par_{2k+1}`, and `Z_{(1)} = Γ'`; by (iv), `dim_F (D_J) = |Γ'|`, and `|Γ'| = Q_k(q)` by part (iii) of `q_theorem_B_lower.md`. ∎

## Checks

- Exact linear algebra over `F_p` for `p ∈ {2, 3, 5, 7}` and enumeration of `T^m`, for every nonempty down-set `Λ` of `Par_m`, `(q, m) ∈ {3} × {0..5} ∪ {5} × {0..4} ∪ {7} × {0..3} ∪ {9} × {0..2}`: 40 cases per prime, `dim V_Λ = |Z_Λ|` in all 160 (including `char F | q − 1`: `p = 2` for every `q`, `p = 3` for `q = 7`).
- Over `F_101`: the 35 cases of `q_induction.md`, equality in all.
- Theorem B: `dim (D_J) = Q_k(q)` for `(k, q) ∈ {(0,3), (0,5), (1,3), (1,5), (1,7), (2,3), (2,5), (3,3)}` over `F_101` (`q_theorem_B_lower.md`).
