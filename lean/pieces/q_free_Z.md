# Freeness over ℤ (Theorem 5.9′, the integral part)

This file uses, unchanged: `q_peeling_lemma.md` (`C_m`), `q_P1_fibres.md` (`λ(M)`, `Z_Λ`), `q_P3_identities.md` (tight patterns and their products), `q_P3_lifts.md` (`Par_m`, down-sets of `Par_m`) and `q_every_field.md` (parts (i) and (iv) of its Theorem: `V_Λ(F)` is spanned by the images of the integer vectors `v_{a,G}`, and `dim_F V_Λ(F) = |Z_Λ|` for every field `F`).

## Setting

Let `q ≥ 3` be odd, `h := (q − 1)/2`, `m ≥ 0`, and `Λ` a down-set of `Par_m`. Let

`C_{m,ℤ} := ℤ[y_1, …, y_m]/(y_1^{q−1}, …, y_m^{q−1})`,

a free abelian group with basis the box monomials `y^a`, `a ∈ B_m := {0, …, q−2}^m`, and let `V_{Λ,ℤ} ⊆ C_{m,ℤ}` be the ideal generated by the tight-pattern products of the elements of `Λ` (the same integer formulas as over a field: `D(y_a, y_b) = Σ_{i=0}^{q−2} (−1)^i y_a^i y_b^{q−2−i}`, `Δ(B) = Π_{c<c'} (y_{b_{c'}} − y_{b_c})`). `|Z_Λ|` is computed with any finite set `T` with a fixed-point-free involution and `|T| = 2h`.

## Lemma (integer matrices)

Let `L ⊆ ℤ^n` be a subgroup (automatically finitely generated). Suppose that for every prime `p` the image of `L` in `F_p^n` has `F_p`-dimension equal to `r := dim_ℚ (ℚ ⊗ L)` (the `ℚ`-dimension of the `ℚ`-span of `L` in `ℚ^n`). Then `ℤ^n / L` is a free abelian group of rank `n − r`.

## Theorem

**(i)** As a subgroup of `C_{m,ℤ}`, `V_{Λ,ℤ}` is the subgroup generated by the vectors `v_{a,G}` (the classes of `y^a·G`, `a ∈ B_m`, `G` a tight-pattern product of an element of `Λ`).

**(ii)** `C_{m,ℤ} / V_{Λ,ℤ}` is a free abelian group of rank `(q − 1)^m − |Z_Λ|`.

## Proofs

*Lemma.* By the structure theorem for subgroups of `ℤ^n` (Smith normal form), there are a basis `e_1, …, e_n` of `ℤ^n` and integers `d_1, …, d_s ≥ 1` (`s ≤ n`) such that `d_1 e_1, …, d_s e_s` is a basis of `L`. Then `s = r` (the `ℚ`-span of `L` has basis `e_1, …, e_s`), and `ℤ^n/L ≅ ⊕_{i≤s} ℤ/d_i ⊕ ℤ^{n−s}`. The image of `L` in `F_p^n` is spanned by the `d_i ē_i`, where the `ē_i` form a basis of `F_p^n`, so its dimension is `#{i ≤ s : p ∤ d_i}`. The hypothesis gives `#{i : p ∤ d_i} = s` for every prime `p`, so no prime divides any `d_i`, i.e. every `d_i = 1`, and `ℤ^n/L ≅ ℤ^{n−r}`.

*Theorem.* (i) `V_{Λ,ℤ}` is the ideal generated by the products `G`; since the box monomials span `C_{m,ℤ}` as a group, the ideal is generated as a group by the `y^a·G`. (ii) Identify `C_{m,ℤ}` with `ℤ^n`, `n := |B_m| = (q − 1)^m`, by the monomial basis, and let `L := V_{Λ,ℤ}`. For every field `F` the map `ℤ → F` sends `L` onto the set of images of the `v_{a,G}`, whose `F`-span is `V_Λ(F)` (part (i) of `q_every_field.md`); its dimension is `|Z_Λ|` (part (iv) of `q_every_field.md`). With `F = ℚ`: `r = |Z_Λ|`; with `F = F_p`: the image of `L` in `F_p^n` has dimension `|Z_Λ| = r`. The Lemma gives `C_{m,ℤ}/V_{Λ,ℤ} ≅ ℤ^{n − |Z_Λ|}`. ∎

## Checks

- Integer Smith normal form of the matrix of the vectors `v_{a,G}`, for every nonempty down-set `Λ` of `Par_m`, `(q, m) ∈ {3} × {1..4} ∪ {5} × {1..3} ∪ {7} × {1..2}`: 19 cases, all elementary divisors equal to `1` and their number equal to `|Z_Λ|`; 0 failures.
- Negative controls of the Smith routine: `[[2,0],[0,1]] → (1, 2)`, `[[6,4],[4,6]] → (2, 10)`, the `3 × 3` circulant `(1,1,0) → (1, 1, 2)`.
