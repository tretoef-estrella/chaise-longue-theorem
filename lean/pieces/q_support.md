# Support of the tight-pattern products, and equality (Theorem 5.9)

This file assembles, unchanged: `q_peeling_lemma.md` (`C_m`), `q_chain_lemma.md` (partitions, `S_t`, `≼`), `q_P1_fibres.md` (the involution on `T`, the residue partition `λ(M)`), `q_P3_identities.md` (`D`, `Δ`, column lengths `λ'_c`, tight patterns, products, `V_Λ`), `q_P3_lifts.md` (`Par_n`, down-sets of `Par_n`), `q_induction.md` (`dim V_Λ ≥ |Z_Λ|`) and `q_degeneration.md` (its Proposition: `dim V ≤ |Σ|` for an ideal generated by homogeneous polynomials).

## Setting

Let `F` be a field, `q ≥ 3` odd, `h := (q − 1)/2`, and `T ⊆ F` a set of `q − 1` elements with `Π_{u∈T}(y − u) = y^{q−1} − 1`. Then `q − 1 ≠ 0` in `F` (the roots are simple), `char F ≠ 2` (as `q − 1` is even, `y^{q−1} − 1` would be a square in characteristic 2), `−1 ∈ T`, and `u ↦ −u` is an involution of `T` without fixed points (`−u = u` would give `2u = 0`). So `T` with `u ↦ −u` satisfies the Setting of `q_P1_fibres.md`, and `Z_Λ := {M ∈ T^m : λ(M) ∈ Λ}`.

The tight-pattern products are images in `C_m` of homogeneous polynomials of `F[y_1, …, y_m]`: `D(y_a, y_b)` is homogeneous of degree `q − 2`, `Δ(B)` of degree `|B|(|B| − 1)/2`, and a product of homogeneous polynomials is homogeneous. We evaluate these polynomials at points `M ∈ T^m` (`y_i ↦ M_i`).

## Lemma (evaluation)

For `a, b ∈ T`:

**(i)** `D(a, b) = 0` if `b ≠ −a`, and `D(a, −a) = −(q − 1) a^{q−2} ≠ 0`.

**(ii)** For a finite set of indices `B`, `Δ(B)(M) ≠ 0` iff the values `M_b`, `b ∈ B`, are pairwise distinct.

**(iii)** Consequently the product `G` of a tight pattern `(P, B_1, …, B_{λ_1})` of `λ` on `{1, …, m}` satisfies `G(M) ≠ 0` iff `M_b = −M_a` for every pair `{a, b} ∈ P` and every block `B_c` carries pairwise distinct values.

## Lemma (support)

If the product of a tight pattern of `λ` on `{1, …, m}` is non-zero at `M ∈ T^m`, then `λ(M) ≼ λ`.

## Theorem (equality, Theorem 5.9 of the paper)

For every `m ≥ 0` and every down-set `Λ` of `Par_m`: `dim_F V_Λ = |Z_Λ|`.

## Proofs

*Evaluation.* (i) `(a + b)·D(a, b) = b^{q−1} − a^{q−1} = 1 − 1 = 0`, so `D(a, b) = 0` if `a + b ≠ 0`. If `b = −a`, every term `(−1)^i a^i (−a)^{q−2−i} = (−1)^{q−2} a^{q−2} = −a^{q−2}` (as `q − 2` is odd), and there are `q − 1` terms. Since `q − 1 ≠ 0` in `F` and `a ≠ 0`, the value is non-zero. (ii) `Δ(B)(M) = Π_{c<c'} (M_{b_{c'}} − M_{b_c})`. (iii) Product of (i) and (ii).

*Support.* Let `G(M) ≠ 0`. Let `M'` be the multiset of the `|λ|` values of `M` at the indices outside the pairs, and `ν'` its multiplicity partition (the multiplicities of the distinct values, in decreasing order).

1. `ν' ≼ λ`. Fix `t ≥ 1` and the `t` most frequent values of `M'`; they occur `S_t(ν')` times. By (iii) a value occurs at most once in each block `B_c`, so these `t` values occupy at most `min(t, λ'_c)` places of `B_c`. Summing over the columns, `S_t(ν') ≤ Σ_c min(t, λ'_c) = S_t(λ)` (the number of boxes of the Young diagram of `λ` in its first `t` rows).
2. `λ(M) ≼ ν'`. By (iii) each pair of `P` is a pair `{u, −u}`, so it adds `1` to both counts of one class and does not change any difference `a − ā`: `λ(M) = λ(M')`. For each class `κ = {u, −u}` unbalanced in `M'`, the part `|a − ā|` of `λ(M')` is at most `max(a, ā)`, which is the multiplicity of one value of `M'`, and distinct classes give distinct values. So the parts of `λ(M)` are bounded, one by one, by distinct parts of `ν'`, which gives `S_t(λ(M)) ≤ S_t(ν')` for every `t`.
3. By transitivity, `λ(M) ≼ λ`. ∎

*Theorem.* `≥` is the Theorem of `q_induction.md`. For `≤`, apply the Proposition of `q_degeneration.md` to the (finitely many) tight-pattern products of the elements of `Λ`, as homogeneous polynomials: `dim V_Λ ≤ |Σ|`, where `Σ` is the set of `M ∈ T^m` at which some product is non-zero. By the support lemma, `M ∈ Σ` gives `λ(M) ≼ λ` for some `λ ∈ Λ`; also `λ(M) ∈ Par_m` (part (i) of `q_P1_fibres.md`, and `|λ(M)| ≤ m`), so `λ(M) ∈ Λ` because `Λ` is a down-set of `Par_m`. Hence `Σ ⊆ Z_Λ` and `dim V_Λ ≤ |Z_Λ|`. ∎
