## 0. Verdict

For **every** `m ≥ 1` and every `k ≥ 0`, Lean 4 proves:

- the ring `R = Z[t_1, …, t_{2k+1}]/(t_1^m − 1, …, t_{2k+1}^m − 1)` modulo the ideal generated by the `(2k+1)!!` elements `ψ_J` of Degtyarev–Shimada is a **free abelian group of rank `m^{2k+1} − Q_k(m)`**;
- for **every field** `F`, `dim_F F[G]/(ψ_J) = m^{2k+1} − Q_k(m)`, and `dim_F (ψ_J) = Q_k(m)`.

Here `Q_k(m) = N!·[x^N] I_0(2x)^{(m−1)/2}` for odd `m` and `Q_k(m) = N!·[x^N] cosh(x)·I_0(2x)^{(m−2)/2}` for even `m`, with `N = 2k+2` and `I_0(2x) = Σ_b x^{2b}/(b!)^2` (paper §1.2). This is Main Theorem′ of the paper (§1.2; Corollary 7.7 for odd `m`, Theorem 9.11 for even `m`), stated there for `m ≥ 3` and `k ≥ 1`. It holds for every prime at once, the prime `2` and the primes dividing `m` included.

Lean 4 also proves **Theorem O with equality** (paper §1.5, §8.7 and Corollary 8.13): for every field `F`, every odd `r ≥ 3` and every `k ≥ 0`, `dim_F (D_{r,J} : J)·F[y_1, …, y_{2k+1}]/(y_i^r) = N!·[x^N] cosh(x)·I_0(2x)^{(r−1)/2}`, and the integral quotient is a free abelian group.

Every module that holds a theorem compiles on the author's Mac (229 of the 233 modules; the other four hold numeric checks only, §2 item 4 and §8). The proof contains no `sorry`, no `admit`, no `native_decide` and no added axiom. Every main theorem depends only on the three standard axioms of Lean and Mathlib: `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

**Main Theorem′ is not the Main Theorem.** The Main Theorem is Conjecture 1.2 itself: `H_{2k}(X; Z)/L(X)` is torsion free. It follows from Main Theorem′ by one topological result, [DS, Theorem 1.1(a)]: the torsion of `R/(ψ_J)` equals the torsion of `H_{2k}(X; Z)/L(X)`. That result rests on Pham's description of the homology of Fermat varieties and on the intersection numbers of [DS, Theorem 2.2]. **It is not formalized; it is cited.** So:

> **Lean certifies the algebraic statement (Main Theorem′) completely, for every degree. The Main Theorem follows from it by one cited topological result, the same one the paper cites.**

The function `Qall` is `Q_k(m)` with the parity split of the paper:

```
def EvenAll.Qall : ℕ → ℕ → ℕ :=
fun m k => if Even m then EvenCount.QkEven k m else TheoremB.Qk k m
def EvenCount.QkEven : ℕ → ℕ → ℕ :=
fun k m =>
  ∑ c ∈ Finset.range (k + 2),
    ∑ b ∈ Fintype.piFinset fun x => Finset.range (2 * k + 3) with 2 * c + 2 * ∑ u, b u = 2 * k + 2,
      (2 * k + 2).factorial / ((2 * c).factorial * ∏ u, (b u).factorial ^ 2)
def TheoremB.Qk : ℕ → ℕ → ℕ :=
fun k q =>
  ∑ b ∈ Fintype.piFinset fun x => Finset.range (2 * k + 3) with 2 * ∑ u, b u = 2 * k + 2,
    (2 * k + 2).factorial / ∏ u, (b u).factorial ^ 2
```

Here `TheoremB.DIdeal F (2h+2) k` is the ideal `(D_{r,J} : J ∈ 𝒥)` of `F[y_1, …, y_{2k+1}]/(y_i^{r})` with `r = 2h+1` (the box ring `Peel.C F q m = F[y]/(y_i^{q−1})` at `q = 2h+2`), `D_r(a, b) = Σ_{u<r} (−1)^u a^u b^{r−1−u}`, and `D_{r,J}` the product of the `D_r(y_a, y_b)` over the pairs of `J` that avoid `0` — the definition `TheoremB.DIdeal` of Theorem B, used here with an even `q`, so that the exponent `q − 1 = r` of the box is odd; `OddEquality.Mideal` is the companion ideal `ℳ = Σ_P D_P·C_N` of Lemma 8.12 over all perfect matchings `P` of `Fin N`. `QkEven k (2h+2)` is the right-hand side `N!·[x^N] cosh(x)·I_0(2x)^{h}` of the paper (row 1 of §1.3). The integral quotients are taken literally by the ideals generated by the integer polynomials `D_J` (`OddEquality.DIdealZ`) and `D_P` (`OddEquality.MidealZ`).

## 2. What is not certified

1. **The topology.** Theorem 0(a), (c), (d) of the paper, i.e. [DS, Theorems 1.1(a) and 1.4], rest on Pham's theorem [Ph] and [DS, Theorem 2.2]. They identify the torsion of `R/(ψ_J)` with that of `H_{2k}(X; Z)/L(X)` and give the rank of `L(X)`. **Not formalized. Mathlib has no singular homology of complex Fermat varieties.**
2. **Hence the Main Theorem itself** — Conjecture 1.2 as a statement about homology — is certified only in its algebraic form, now for every degree.
3. **Not formalized, and not on the path of the proof:**
   - Corollary H (the integral Hodge conjecture) and Corollary W (partial Fermat varieties);
   - Theorem A of §10 and the counterexample of §11;
   - the verification record of §14;
   - the rest of §10 (Proposition 10.5, Corollaries 10.6 and 10.8), Proposition 2.6, the topological half of Proposition 2.1, and Lemma 12.4;
   - in Lemma 8.12, the injectivity of `θ` and the equality of its image (only the inclusion used is formalized);
   - [DS, Claim 4.3] for a subfamily `K ≠ 𝒥` (only `K = 𝒥` is formalized);
   - the graded refinement of Theorem B and of Theorem O (the dimension of each graded piece does not depend on `F`): optional in the pieces, not done.
   - *Formalized although not needed:* Theorems 5.9 and 5.9′, the `q = 3` case of §4, Theorem O with equality (§1.4), Corollaries 7.8 and 9.12, and the case `r = 3` of Theorem O without Pfaffians (Theorem 8.15).
4. **Two modules of numeric checks are not compiled on the author's Mac.** `EvenAll.Checks` (piece E20) and `OddEquality.Checks` (piece E15) evaluate counts such as `Q_k(m)` at the paper's cells by `decide`. ### 3.1 The chain of the final theorem, even `m`

Every arrow is a Lean proof.

1. **Upper bound, every field** (E2, `finrank_IK_le_even`): `dim_F I_F ≤ Q_k(m)`, by the route of version 1 — `dim_F I_F = rank_F(A)` for the integer matrix `A`; `rank_F ≤ rank_ℚ`; over `ℂ`, evaluation at the `m`-th roots of unity, now with the root `−1`, gives `|Γ|`; and `|Γ| = Q_k(m)` (Lemma 9.1). This is [DS, Claim 4.3] for even `m`, re-proved without topology.
2. **Lower bound at a prime `p | m`, every cofactor** (E19, `EvenOne.E3`), over the algebraic closure of `F_p`, with `m = p^v·r'`, `p ∤ r'`:
   - `dim I = Σ_c dim π_c(I)` over the colourings `c` (Lemma 9.4 = Lemma 6.1 with no parity, E16);
   - each term is a product of block dimensions (Lemma 9.5, E16), now with the self-inverse colours `1` and `−1`;
   - the count factors in the same way, with sum `Q_k(m)` (Lemma 9.6, E17);
   - each block dimension is at least its count: the pair blocks by Theorem C for every box `q = p^v`, the prime `2` included (Lemma 9.7, E18 with E1); the colour `1` at an odd prime by Theorem B (Lemma 9.8, E18); the colour `−1` at an odd prime by Theorem O at the box `p^v` (Lemma 9.9, E18 with E14); the colour `1` at the prime `2` by Proposition 9.2 and Theorem O at the box `2^v − 1` (Lemma 9.10, E19 with E4 and E14).
3. **Theorem O, `≥`** (E14, `OddTheorem.theoremO`), from the Pfaffians and patterns of E6–E13.
4. **`H(m, k)` for every even `m`** (E20, `A3`). `H(m, k)` is `EvenAssembly.HypH m k`: for every prime `p | m`, the rank over `F_p` of the integer matrix `A` is `Q_k(m)`. Steps 1 and 2 give `rank(A) = Q_k(m)` over the algebraic closure of `F_p`, and the rank does not change from `F_p` to its algebraic closure (`rank_map_algebraMap`, version 1).
5. **Every field and freeness over `Z`** (E5 with E20: `EvenAssembly.theorem_ii`, `theorem_iii`; `EvenAll.B1`, `B2`): `rank_F(A)` depends only on the prime field; characteristic `0` and `p ∤ m` from step 1 and the count of `Γ`; `p | m` from `H(m, k)`; freeness by the integer-matrix lemma of version 1 (piece 14).
6. **Every `m ≥ 1`** (E20, `mainTheorem'`): odd `m` by version 1, even `m` by step 5.

## 5. The procedure

The procedure of version 1 (§5 there), applied to each of the 20 pieces, with four additions learned on the way:

1. **The whole cycle in one turn.** When a piece came back: download, diff, grep, build, check file, axioms, faithfulness audit, register; then write the next piece, brute-force it, send it, register the send.
2. **One module at a time under a watchdog** (`vigia.sh`): every local `lake build` of a module ran alone, with a cap of 4.5 GB of real memory and 900 s; the cap was never raised.
3. **A check file per piece** (`checks/CheckE<N>.lean`) restates the main statements **by hand**, with every type written out, and Lean must accept the restatement against the proved theorem. A restatement that does not typecheck stops the cycle.
4. **Controls that can fail.** Every brute-force check before sending carries negative controls (a deliberately wrong variant); a control that cannot fire (for instance one that negates a statement true in every cell tested) was replaced, and the silent ones are declared in the logs.

**Stop conditions, fixed in advance:** a property NEGATED by Aristotle; a statement that is not faithful; a build failure; a `sorry` or an extra axiom; a change to an old file. **None occurred**, apart from the two modules of numeric checks that exceeded the memory cap of the local build (§2 item 4); they contain no theorem used elsewhere.

**«Review suggested».** Aristotle's page showed this badge on several pieces (among them E15, E18, E19, E20). Every time it accompanied Aristotle's own list of formalization choices: hypotheses that were dropped (statements more general than asked), a different but equivalent form, an added hypothesis stated openly, or parts of a piece weaker than written but sufficient for the argument. Each was read and judged in the audit log. In E15 the choices were: (C3) needs `T` nonempty (true in the use, `0 ∈ T`); (C1) stated for an arbitrary finite point set; (C5) proved as a surjection, which is all the argument needs; the optional graded statement (D3) not done.

## 10. What this certificate claims, in one paragraph

A computer proof assistant, Lean 4 with the Mathlib library, has checked a complete proof of the following statement, with no gap and no axiom beyond the three standard ones:

> For every `m ≥ 1` and every `k ≥ 0`, the quotient of the integral group ring `Z[(Z/m)^{2k+1}]` by the ideal generated by the Degtyarev–Shimada elements `ψ_J` is a free abelian group of rank `m^{2k+1} − Q_k(m)`, and over every field the corresponding quotient has that dimension.

By [DS, Theorem 1.1(a)] (Pham's theorem and the intersection numbers of [DS, Theorem 2.2], cited and not formalized), this is equivalent to Conjecture 1.2 of Degtyarev and Shimada for the Fermat varieties of degree `m` in even dimension `2k`, for every `m ≥ 3`.

The proofs in Lean were written by the AI system Aristotle (Harmonic) from pieces written from the paper. The audit, by the author assisted by Claude (Anthropic), checked that the statements match the paper, and compiled and re-checked everything on the author's machine. Before release, a separate reader read this certificate cold, compared the statements with the paper and with [DS] in the original, and re-checked the compiled proofs through the Lean kernel; its verdict was «holds as far as I can check».
