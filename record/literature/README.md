# The literature search of version 12

§1.10 of the paper (version 12) says what we searched for, where, and what we found. This folder is the record behind it.

- [`LITERATURA_V12_v1.md`](LITERATURA_V12_v1.md) ([pdf](LITERATURA_V12_v1.pdf)) — the sweep, written as it went (3–4 October 2026): a table of every result we call new or attribute, with the source read, where we searched, and the verdict; and, at the end, what each original added. It is a working document: some verdicts of its first table were revised after the originals were read, and the revisions are written below the lines they revise. **The paper, not this file, states the final attributions.**
- [`QUERIES.md`](QUERIES.md) — the query log: every search, with its date, its count of hits and what was opened.

**Where we searched:** the arXiv listings and its index of titles and abstracts; Google Scholar and the open web; the papers that cite [DS], through Semantic Scholar and OpenAlex; the publication lists of the authors of [DS]; MathOverflow; and GitHub, including Mathlib and DeepMind's `formal-conjectures` repository.
