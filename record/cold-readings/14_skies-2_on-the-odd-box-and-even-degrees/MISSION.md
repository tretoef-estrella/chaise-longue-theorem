> **Proposition B5.** For even `m ≥ 4` and `k ≥ 1`, Conjecture 1.2 of Degtyarev–Shimada at `(k, m)` follows from the statement of Theorem O at the boxes `r = p^v` in characteristic `p` (for each odd prime power `p^v ∥ m`) and `r = 2^v − 1` in characteristic `2` (if `2^v ∥ m`, `v ≥ 2`), for all smaller `k` as well.
