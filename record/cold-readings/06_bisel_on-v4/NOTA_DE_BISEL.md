## Auditor's reading
- Agree on the substance. Two corrections of scope, to keep the paper honest:
  1. For k = 1 the conjecture was already a theorem of Degtyarev [De14] (the paper says so in §1.1). «Closed for an infinite family in every even dimension» is new for k ≥ 2 only.
  2. «No argument local-to-global can exist» is stronger than Fact 7.2; §7 says: no argument valid for ALL subfamilies.
- The Bessel/random-walk line is decoration: it is the generating function of the count, not an application. Keep it out of the paper.
- Journals: Duke is a reach without a human endorsement; Compositio / a strong specialist journal is the realistic target. The decision should wait for the Fable-4 reading and, above all, for a human expert.
