## 0. The claim you audit

The document is `material/certificate/LEAN_CERTIFICATE_CHAISE_LONGUE_v2.md` (and its `.pdf`). It says that Lean 4 with Mathlib proves **Main Theorem′** of the paper *The Chaise Longue Theorem*, version 12 (`material/paper/PAPER_OFICIAL_v12.md`), for **every degree `m ≥ 1`**. Its final theorem, `EvenAll.mainTheorem'`, says:

> for every `m ≥ 1` and every `k ≥ 0`, the quotient of `Z[t_1, …, t_{2k+1}]/(t_i^m − 1)` by the ideal generated by the `(2k+1)!!` elements `ψ_J` of Degtyarev–Shimada is a free abelian group of rank `m^{2k+1} − Q_k(m)`; and for every field `F`, `dim_F F[G]/(ψ̄_J) = m^{2k+1} − Q_k(m)` and `dim_F (ψ̄_J) = Q_k(m)`.

It also claims **Theorem O with equality**: `OddEquality.D1`, `D2_DJ`, `D2_M`. It claims that the proof has no `sorry` and that it depends only on the axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. It says plainly that the topology of [DS] is not formalized.

The Lean proofs were written by an AI system (Aristotle, by Harmonic) and compiled again on the author's machine. **The Lean kernel checks the proofs. Nobody checks by machine that the Lean statement says what the paper says.** That is your main job.

A machine-checked proof of the wrong statement is worth nothing. A vacuous definition can make a statement true for the wrong reason. A wrong number in the certificate costs the author credibility. **Look for the line that allows a rejection.** If you find none, say so plainly and say how hard you looked.

### M2. Vacuity and Mathlib traps

- `Module.finrank` is `0` for a module that is not finite. Can «rank `= m^{2k+1} − Q_k(m)`» hold vacuously, for instance when the right side is `0`? Is finiteness proved somewhere, or does it follow from the statement?
- Natural subtraction truncates at `0`. Is `m ^ (2k+1) − Qall m k` ever truncated? The certificate says no: check its argument.
- Edge cases `m = 1`, `m = 2`, `k = 0`: what do the definitions give, and is that what the paper means?
- Is «every field» really every field: any characteristic, `2` and the divisors of `m` included, and every universe?
- Is any hypothesis impossible to satisfy, any type empty, any quantifier over the wrong set?
- **Evaluate, do not only read.** Compute `Qall m k` in Lean at several cells, for example `(m, k) = (3,1), (4,1), (5,1), (6,1), (4,2), (7,1), (8,1), (9,1)`. `EvenCount.QkEven` and `TheoremB.Qk` can be evaluated with `#eval`. `EvenAll.Qall` is declared `noncomputable` (Lean's `#print` does not show that word), so prove its values with `decide` or `simp`, or go through the other two. Compute the same numbers independently in Python from [DS, Definition 1.3], by counting the set `Γ` directly, not by the paper's formula. They must agree.
- Then derive in Lean one **non-vacuous instance of your own choosing**, other than the certificate's `m = 4, k = 1`. For example: the rank at `m = 6, k = 1`, or the dimension over `ZMod 3` at `m = 3, k = 1`, from `EvenAll.mainTheorem'` itself.
