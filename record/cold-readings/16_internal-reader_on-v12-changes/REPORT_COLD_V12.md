### P1. §8.3: the definition; (i), (ii), (iii) with `ε(x, z)`; «over every commutative ring, characteristic 2 included» (lines 829–835)

*Definition.* `Pf(A) = Σ_π sgn(π) Π_{x<y ∈ π} a_{xy}`, `sgn(π)` the sign of the listing permutation. Independence of the order of the pairs: exchanging two pairs in the listing is an even permutation (two transpositions). «It is `(−1)` to the number of crossings»: the listing is obtained from the identity by moving elements; each pair of pairs `{a<b}, {x<y}` with `a < x` contributes the inversions among `(a, b, x, y)` listed as `a b x y`: none if `b < x` (disjoint) or `x < y < b` (nested: `b>x`, `b>y` — two inversions), one if `x < b < y` (crossing). So the parity is the number of crossings. Checked on all 1 070 matchings with ≤ 10 points (T1, control with nestings fires 468 times).

*(i)* A simultaneous permutation `σ` of rows and columns: the matchings are carried bijectively, the products are carried to the same products, and the listing signs change by `sgn(σ)`. Holds over any ring (combinatorial sign only). T2: 175/175.

*(ii) — the point of the item.* Let `x ≠ x'` have equal rows: `a_{xz} = a_{x'z}` for every `z` (so `a_{xx'} = a_{x'x'} = 0`). Matchings pairing `x` with `x'` contribute `±a_{xx'}·(…) = 0`. On the others, `τ := (x x')` acts: if `π ∋ {x, y}, {x', y'}` with `y ≠ y'`, then `τπ ∋ {x', y}, {x, y'}`; `τπ ≠ π` (else `y = y'`), so `τ` is a **fixed-point-free** involution on this set. Its weight: list `π` as a word, apply `τ` to the letters: the new listing is the old one composed with a transposition, so its sign is opposite; the new product replaces `a_{xy}` by `a_{x'y} = a_{xy}` and `a_{x'y'}` by `a_{xy'} = a_{x'y'}` (equal rows; the weight `sgn·product` does not depend on the order inside a pair, since both factors change sign together). So the terms cancel in pairs `w + (−w) = 0`. **No division by 2 is used**: the cancellation is `w − w = 0`, valid in every commutative ring, characteristic 2 included. (The v11 argument «`Pf = −Pf`» needed no 2-torsion; the new one does not.) T3: over `Z`, and with matrices whose rows are equal only modulo 2 (resp. 4), `Pf ≡ 0 mod 2` (resp. mod 4) in 210/210 cases each; the control (rows broken by one unit) gives a non-zero Pfaffian 77 (mod 2) and 86 (mod 4) times.

*(iii)* Group the matchings by the partner `z` of `x`. With positions counted from 0, moving `x, z` to the front of the listing costs `p_x + p_z − 1` transpositions if `x < z` and `p_x + p_z` if `z < x` (after `x` moves to the front, `z` sits at `p_z + 1`). So `ε(x, z)` is as printed. The remainder is a matching of `X ∖ {x, z}` with its own listing sign. Holds in every ring. «Additive in the row-and-column of `x`»: `Pf(A^{x,z})` does not involve the row of `x`. T4: 300/300; the control (branches exchanged) fails 292 times.

*«Every commutative ring».* The three proofs are combinatorial regroupings and one pairwise cancellation; the identities with integer coefficients (iii) hold for the generic alternating matrix over `Z` and specialize. **HOLDS.**

### M1. §8.5 (a): `ω_s`, antisymmetry with no division by 2, `ω_{r−2} = D^−`, the second item, (8.6) (lines 885–894)

- *Antisymmetry.* The index set `U_s = {u : 0 ≤ u ≤ r−1, 0 ≤ s−u ≤ r−1}` is stable under `u ↦ s−u`. `ω_s(b, a) = Σ_u (−1)^u b^u a^{s−u} = Σ_{u'} (−1)^{s−u'} a^{u'} b^{s−u'} = −ω_s(a, b)` (`s` odd). `ω_s(a, a) = Σ_{u ∈ U_s} (−1)^u a^s`: the involution has no fixed point (`u = s − u` would make `s` even) and pairs terms of opposite sign, so the sum is `0` **term by term, without dividing by 2** ✓.
- `s = r − 2`: `s − u ≤ r − 1` always, `s − u ≥ 0` iff `u ≤ r − 2`; so `ω_{r−2} = Σ_{u=0}^{r−2}(−1)^u a^u b^{r−2−u} = D^−` ✓.
