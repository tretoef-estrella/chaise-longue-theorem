# How to cite

## The paper

> Amichis Luengo, R. (2026). *The Chaise Longue Theorem: Conjecture 1.2 of Degtyarev–Shimada for the Fermat varieties of every odd degree in every even dimension, and the integral Hodge conjecture for those whose degree is prime or prime to (n+2)!* (Preprint, version v10). Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22961150

```bibtex
@misc{AmichisLuengo2026ChaiseLongue,
  author       = {Amichis Luengo, Rafael},
  title        = {The Chaise Longue Theorem: Conjecture 1.2 of Degtyarev--Shimada for the
                  Fermat Varieties of Every Odd Degree in Every Even Dimension, and the
                  Integral Hodge Conjecture for Those Whose Degree Is Prime or Prime to (n+2)!},
  year         = {2026},
  month        = oct,
  howpublished = {Preprint, version v10},
  publisher    = {Zenodo},
  doi          = {10.5281/zenodo.22961150},
  url          = {https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22961150}
}
```

- **The DOI above is the concept DOI**: it always resolves to the latest version. To cite version v10 exactly, use **10.5281/zenodo.23087729** (v9: 10.5281/zenodo.23085319; v8: 10.5281/zenodo.23045409; v7: 10.5281/zenodo.22961151).
- **The surname is *Amichis Luengo*** (two surnames, Spanish usage). Please keep them together and in this order: *Amichis Luengo, R.* in a reference list, *Amichis Luengo* in the text — not *Luengo, R. A.*

## This repository (the working record)

> Amichis Luengo, R. (2026). *The Chaise Longue Theorem — working record* [Data set, software and documents]. GitHub. https://github.com/tretoef-estrella/chaise-longue-theorem

```bibtex
@misc{AmichisLuengo2026ChaiseLongueRecord,
  author       = {Amichis Luengo, Rafael},
  title        = {The Chaise Longue Theorem --- working record},
  year         = {2026},
  howpublished = {GitHub repository},
  url          = {https://github.com/tretoef-estrella/chaise-longue-theorem}
}
```

GitHub also offers a ready citation in the sidebar (*Cite this repository*), generated from [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff).

## A standalone document

Every file in [theorems/standalone/](theorems/INDEX.md) carries its own citation line in its header, of the form:

> Amichis Luengo, R. (2026). *Title of the document* [working document]. In *The Chaise Longue Theorem — working record*. https://github.com/tretoef-estrella/chaise-longue-theorem/blob/main/theorems/standalone/FILE.md

Those documents are working records and have not been refereed; where a statement also appears in the paper, please cite the paper.

## The earlier campaigns

- *The Sofa Theorem* (the case $k = 2$): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21288551 — [repository](https://github.com/tretoef-estrella/sofa-theorem)
- *The Hammock Theorem* (the case $k = 3$): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21382543 — [repository](https://github.com/tretoef-estrella/hammock-theorem)

Both were written under an identification that the Chaise Longue paper shows to be false (§1.8); their conclusions about Conjecture 1.2 are the cases $k = 2, 3$ of the Main Theorem, proved there by a different route.
