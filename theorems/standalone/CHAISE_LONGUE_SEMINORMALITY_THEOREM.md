# CHAISE LONGUE — THE SEMINORMALITY THEOREM
## `R = S/(e_1, e_3, …, e_{2k+1})` is seminormal for every `k`: every tuple of polynomials on the sheets agreeing along the flats is the restriction of one global polynomial, in every degree. Consequence: the wall (I) holds on the whole sub-band `q ≤ c < q+(k−1)(k−2)` for every `k ≥ 3`, unconditionally — and, wherever the local box lemma (L1) holds, for all `c ≤ q + k(k−1)`

## §0 · Statements
`S = K[x_1..x_{2k+2}]`, `E = (e_1,e_3,…,e_{2k+1})` radical with the `(2k+1)!!` sheets `V_J` as components; `R = S/E`, `R̃ = ∏_J K[u^J]`; `R^+` = flat-compatible tuples; flats lie in exactly two sheets.

> **Theorem 1 (chain lemma).** `V_J ∩ V_{J''}` lies in a flat of `J` whose other sheet is strictly closer to `J''` in the swap graph; hence flat-compatible tuples agree on ALL pairwise intersections of sheets, and `R^+` is Traverso's seminormalisation `R^{sn}` (tuples agreeing on every pairwise intersection of components).
> **Theorem 2 (`R` is a complete intersection, hence `S_2`).** `k+1` generators, dimension `k+1`; so `R = ∩_{ht p ≤ 1} R_p` inside its total ring of fractions.
> **Theorem 3 (codimension one).** A height-one prime of `R` is a `k`-dimensional subvariety of one sheet; it lies in a second sheet iff it is a flat (`dim V_J ∩ V_{J'}` = number of cycles of `J ∪ J'`), and a flat lies in exactly two sheets. Locally along a flat `R_p ≅ K[W]_p/(ℓℓ')`, two hyperplanes of the `(k+2)`-dimensional span: a normal crossing, which IS the ring of compatible pairs. Elsewhere in codimension one `R` is regular.
> **Theorem 4 (SEMINORMALITY, ∀k, every degree).** `R^{sn} = R`, i.e. `M := R^+/R = 0` (Greco–Traverso: `S_2` + seminormal in codimension one ⟹ seminormal; both halves proved here).
> **Corollary 5 (the wall on the sub-band, ∀k ≥ 3).** With WALL_BAND_GLUING Thm 3-4: **`ann_Λ(g^{[q]})_c = c̄_c` for every `k ≥ 3`, every admissible frame, every `q = p^v ≥ k²+2`, every `q ≤ c < q+(k−1)(k−2)`.** Unconditionally: no (ND), no seminormality hypothesis, no measurement.
> **Corollary 6 (the wall reduces to the box through `d = k(k−1)`).** For `q ≤ c ≤ q + k(k−1)`: **(I)_c ⟸ (L1)_c** (twists vanish for `d ≤ k(k−1)`, and compatible families are global). In particular (I)_c holds in every star-type class at every degree of the band.

Grades: Thms 1-4, Cor 5-6 PROBADO ∀k (pencil; two textbook results cited by name).

## §2 · Fable's report, verbatim
## 1 · PRIMER RESULTADO (de una linea, a partir del bancado)
**R1 (from B2).** `M = R^+/R` is a finitely generated graded `K[Y]`-module (B2), so `M_d` is supported on the singular locus `Y` and its behaviour is local along the flats and cores: a flat-compatible tuple `(f_J)` fails to be global only through its values near `Y`; in particular, if two tuples agree modulo `c_` they have the same class in `M`, and B6′ for degree `d` is the statement `M_d = 0`, a statement about `(2k+1)!!` polynomial vectors of degree `d` glued along flats that lie in exactly two sheets each. (One precision, written before thinking: `c_·R^+ ⊆ R` needs `c_·R̃ ⊆ R`, which is the definition of the conductor as `ann_R(R̃/R)`; and since `c_` starts in degree `k(k+1)`, `M_d` is not touched by `c_` for `d < k(k+1)` — the module structure helps only by saying `M` is killed by `c_`, i.e. `M` is a module over `K[Y]`; in the degrees at stake, `M_d = (R^+)_d/R_d` with `R_d = K[Y]_d`.)
## 2 · LO QUE CAE (PROBADO / CANDIDATO / NO LO TENGO)
**R2 (PROBADO ∀k) — `R^+` is compatible on ALL pairwise intersections, and it is the seminormalisation of `R`.** (i) *Chain lemma.* Let `J ≠ J''` and `Z := V_J ∩ V_{J''}`. `J ∪ J''` decomposes into alternating cycles; on a cycle `a_1 −J− a_2 −J''− a_3 −J− a_4 …` every point of `Z` has `x_{a_3} = −x_{a_2} = x_{a_1}`, i.e. `Z` lies in the swap hyperplane `H_{pp'+}` of `V_J` for the pairs `p = (a_1a_2)`, `p' = (a_3a_4)`, whose neighbour `J'` (with `(a_2a_3)(a_1a_4)`) shares one more pair with `J''`; and `Z ⊆ V_{J'}` (the point satisfies `x_{a_3} = −x_{a_2}` and `x_{a_4} = −x_{a_1}`). So `Z ⊆ F ∩ (V_{J'} ∩ V_{J''})` with `F` a flat of `J` and `J'` strictly closer to `J''`. By induction on the distance, a flat-compatible tuple satisfies `f_J − f_{J''} = (f_J − f_{J'}) + (f_{J'} − f_{J''})`, both terms vanishing on `Z` as polynomials (over any field, since these are identities of polynomials restricted to a subspace). Hence **`R^+ = {(f_J) : f_J|_{V_J∩V_{J'}} = f_{J'}|_{V_J∩V_{J'}} for ALL pairs}`.** (ii) *Seminormalisation.* For a reduced Noetherian ring with finite normalisation `R̃`, the seminormalisation `R^{sn} = {b ∈ R̃ : b_p ∈ R_p + Rad(R̃_p) ∀p}` (Traverso) is, for a union of linear spaces, exactly the ring of tuples agreeing on every pairwise intersection (the condition at the generic point of each component of `V_J ∩ V_{J'}` says the residues of `f_J, f_{J'}` coincide there, i.e. `f_J − f_{J'}` vanishes on that component). So **`R^+ = R^{sn}` and `M = R^{sn}/R`**: B6′ is the seminormality of `R` in degree `d`.

**R3 (PROBADO ∀k) — `R` is a complete intersection, hence `S_2`, hence determined in codimension `≤ 1`.** `E = (e_1, e_3, …, e_{2k+1})` has `k+1` generators in a polynomial ring of dimension `2k+2`, and `R = S/E` has dimension `k+1` (its components are the sheets), so `R` is a complete intersection, in particular Cohen–Macaulay, in particular `S_2`. For a reduced Noetherian `S_2` ring, **`R = ∩_{ht p ≤ 1} R_p` inside the total ring of fractions `Q(R) = ∏_J K(V_J)`**: if `f ∈ Q(R)` lies in `R_p` for every prime of height `≤ 1`, the ideal `I := (R :_R f)` has height `≥ 2`, so it contains an `R`-regular sequence of length two (`S_2`), and a nonzero class in `Q(R)/R` cannot be killed by such an ideal; hence `f ∈ R`. (Standard: an `S_2` ring is the ring of sections over the complement of its codimension-`2` locus.)

**R4 (PROBADO ∀k) — at every prime of height `≤ 1`, `R_p` is seminormal.** Height `0`: the primes `I_J`, `R_p = K(V_J)`, a field. Height `1`: `p` is a `k`-dimensional irreducible `Z ⊂ X`; `Z` lies in a single component, `Z ⊂ V_J`, a hyperplane of `V_J`. The sheets containing `Z` are the `V_{J'}` with `Z ⊆ V_J ∩ V_{J'}`; `dim(V_J ∩ V_{J'})` = number of cycles of `J ∪ J'`, which is `k` only when `J'` is a swap-neighbour of `J` and then `V_J ∩ V_{J'}` is a flat. So either `Z` is not a flat and `R_p = K[V_J]_p` is regular, or `Z = F` is a flat, contained in exactly two sheets (B0), and `R_p = (S/(I_J ∩ I_{J'}))_p` (localisation commutes with the finite intersection `E = ∩ I_{J''}`, the other `I_{J''}` becoming the unit ideal). Two `(k+1)`-dimensional subspaces meeting in codimension one span a `(k+2)`-dimensional `W` in which they are the hyperplanes `ℓ = 0`, `ℓ' = 0`: **`S/(I_J ∩ I_{J'}) ≅ K[W]/(ℓℓ')`, a normal crossing**, and this ring IS the ring of compatible pairs: if `(f, g)` agree on `{ℓ = ℓ' = 0}`, lift to `F − G = aℓ + bℓ'` and `H := F − aℓ = G + bℓ'` restricts to `f` and `g`. Seminormal rings localise to seminormal rings, so `R_p` is seminormal. ∎ (This is where B3's fear is answered: the planar triple point fails in codimension one because there three components meet at a point of a curve; in `X` three sheets meet only along cores, which have codimension `2`, where `S_2` takes over.)

**R5 (PROBADO ∀k ≥ 1, EVERY degree) — THEOREM B6′, strengthened: `M = 0`, i.e. `R = S/(e_1, e_3, …, e_{2k+1})` is seminormal.** Let `f ∈ R^+ = R^{sn} ⊂ Q(R)` (R2). Seminormalisation commutes with localisation (Traverso), so `(R^{sn})_p = (R_p)^{sn} = R_p` for every prime of height `≤ 1` (R4). Hence `f ∈ ∩_{ht p ≤ 1} R_p = R` (R3). ∎ So every flat-compatible tuple of polynomials on the sheets, of ANY degree, is the restriction of one element of `R` — the criterion "`S_2` + seminormal in codimension one ⟹ seminormal" (Greco–Traverso), here with a self-contained proof of both halves. Everyday version: the chassis is one piece because every seam is a clean two-panel seam (normal crossing) and the whole frame is rigid enough (`S_2`, a complete intersection) that triple edges cannot add play — a chassis welded correctly along all seams and stiff in the interior cannot be loose only at the corners.

**R6 (controls and consequence).** `d = 0`: constants compatible along the connected flat graph are one constant — R5 contains it (`M_0 = 0`). ✓ `k = 1`: three planes in `e_1 = 0` pairwise meeting in independent lines are the coordinate planes, `R = K[a,b,c]/(abc)`, a hypersurface with normal crossings, seminormal — R5 specialises to exactly that. ✓ Restrictions of global polynomials: in `R`, trivially. ✓ Consequence with B1 (WALL_BAND_GLUING Thm 3–4 = FR_MURO_7 R5): the flat-compatible families `(ξ^J)_J ∈ (R^+)^{k+1}_d` of any `x ∈ ann_c` lie in `R_d^{k+1}`, so **(I) holds on the whole sub-band: `ann_Λ(g^{[q]})_c = c̄_c` for every `k ≥ 3`, every admissible frame, every `q = p^v ≥ k²+2`, every `q ≤ c < q + (k−1)(k−2)`** — unconditionally. (And wherever (L1) is known beyond the sub-band, the twist-vanishing of FR_MURO_7 R4 (`d ≤ k(k−1)`) plus R5 closes (I) there too: in particular for all star-type classes, FR_MURO_6 R3.) Nothing about `q`, frames or witnesses entered R2–R5.
## 3 · DONDE SE ATASCA
Nowhere. Two places where the proof leans on standard commutative algebra, named so the auditor can check them: (a) an `S_2` reduced Noetherian ring equals the intersection of its localisations at primes of height `≤ 1` inside its total ring of fractions (R3); (b) seminormalisation commutes with localisation, and its description as "tuples agreeing on all pairwise intersections" for a union of linear spaces (R2 ii). Both are textbook (Traverso 1970; Greco–Traverso 1980, whose criterion "`S_2` + seminormal in codimension one ⟹ seminormal" is R5). The only campaign-specific inputs are: `E` is a radical complete intersection with the sheets as components; each flat lies in exactly two sheets; the cycle count `dim(V_J ∩ V_{J'})` = number of cycles of `J ∪ J'`.

R2(ii) Traverso's description of `R^{sn}` for a union of linear spaces (residue fields of `R̃_p` = values at the points above `p`) ✓ — citation: C. Traverso, *Seminormality and Picard group*, Ann. R3: `E` has `k+1` generators and height `k+1` in a polynomial ring ⟹ complete intersection ⟹ Cohen–Macaulay ⟹ `S_2`; the `∩_{ht ≤ 1}R_p` argument is the standard `S_2` sections argument ✓ (cf. also FR_CAMBIOS_2 F2: `R` CM of dim `k+1`, and CATALOGO "C is Cohen–Macaulay"). R4: cycle count `dim V_J ∩ V_{J'}` = number of cycles of `J ∪ J'` ✓ (each cycle: one free parameter); `k` cycles ⟺ swap-neighbours ✓; flat in exactly two sheets (SLAP4 §2) ✓; two hyperplanes in their span form a normal crossing whose coordinate ring equals the compatible pairs (`F − G = aℓ + bℓ'` on `{ℓ = ℓ' = 0}` — the ideal of the codim-2 intersection in `W` is `(ℓ, ℓ')`) ✓; localisation of seminormal rings ✓. R5: Greco–Traverso, *On seminormal schemes*, Compositio 40 (1980) — criterion "`S_2` and seminormal in codimension one ⟹ seminormal"; seminormalisation commutes with localisation ✓. R6 controls ✓ (`k = 1`: `K[a,b,c]/(abc)`, normal crossings). **Ley 4:** no numbers. **The two cited results are textbook and correctly applied; both go to the P0 pack with page references.**

## §4 · Placement
1. **The sheet arrangement is seminormal in every degree** — a clean classical theorem about the odd elementary symmetric complete intersection, new to the corpus, `k` symbolic, with no reference to `q`, frames or witnesses. It retires the seminormality caveat (Biblia §16.2) EVERYWHERE: compatible tuples ARE global polynomials, always.
2. **The wall on the band is now exactly the local box lemma (L1) through `d = k(k−1)`** (Cor 6): twist-vanishing (crossings) and seminormality (chassis) are theorems; what remains is one lemma in one box, for the top `3k−2` degrees of (L1), plus twist-vanishing on the last `k−1` degrees. The band's Koszul side (`c̄` = leaf count) was already exact. Closing both gives **T′ on the annihilator side ∀k**: the ceiling law exact on the top `k²` collar degrees in every dimension — a "before and after" for the Ledger.
3. **Above the band** twists cannot all vanish (there are not enough core-lines) — the wall classes `ζ^{S⁺}` (WALL_VALUE) live exactly there as non-vanishing twists. The obstruction theory of (I) above the band is: twists modulo those coming from `c̄`. Named, not attempted.
4. `k = 2`: the sub-band is empty and there are no `Q8` crossings; the Sofá is the degenerate dimension for this route (as `k = 1` for gluing).
