## 1 · Proof of (A) — field invariance

`E` is generated by the elementary symmetric polynomials of odd index and `m^{[q]}` by monomials;
all coefficients lie in the prime field `F_3`. Fix `d`. The degree-`d` piece of `E + m^{[q]}` is the
`K`-span of the finite set `\{x^α e_j\}_{j\ \mathrm{odd}} ∪ \{x^α x_i^q\}` of degree `d`, whose coordinate
matrix `G_d` in the monomial basis has **entries in `F_3`**. Row reduction of an `F_3`-matrix chooses
its pivots inside `F_3` and those pivots remain pivots over any extension, so
`\mathrm{rank}_K(G_d ⊗ K) = \mathrm{rank}_{F_3}(G_d)`. Hence

> `\dim_K A_d = \dim_K S_d − \mathrm{rank}_K(G_d ⊗ K) = \dim_{F_3}(S_{F_3})_d − \mathrm{rank}_{F_3}(G_d)`,

independent of `K`. The identical argument applies to `R`, to `Λ` once its defining forms are fixed,
and to `Φ_T`, whose matrix is the Fedder matrix with entries `±1` (draft Theorem 6.4). ∎

*This is Lemma 5.1 of the assembly draft, restated here with its consequence made explicit.*

**Corollary A1.** The four Ledger terms `ZA_k(q)`, `W_k(q)`, `Collar_k(q)`, `Top_k(q)` are sums of
`\dim_K A_d` over `q`-determined ranges of `d`, hence field-independent integers. `P_k(q)` is a count
of `F_q`-points, hence an integer that does not refer to `K` at all. **Therefore any identity among
these quantities, once proved over one field of characteristic `3`, holds over every field of
characteristic `3`.**

## 2 · Proof of (B) — perfectness is the right hypothesis

Let `K` be perfect of characteristic `3` and `q = 3^v`. Write `Fr(f) := f^q`; `Fr` is additive and
injective on `K[u]`, and `Fr(cf) = c^q Fr(f)`.

**Unique factorisation of monomials.** Every monomial `m = ∏_j u_j^{a_j}` factors uniquely as
`m = Fr(m')·ρ` with `m' = ∏ u_j^{\lfloor a_j/q \rfloor}` and `ρ = ∏ u_j^{a_j \bmod q}` `q`-reduced —
division with remainder on each exponent.

**Existence.** Let `μ = Σ_m c_m m`. Grouping by the remainder `ρ`,
`μ = Σ_ρ \big( Σ_{m : \mathrm{rem}(m) = ρ} c_m\,Fr(m') \big)ρ`. Because `K` is perfect, each `c_m` has a
(unique) `q`-th root `c_m^{1/q} ∈ K`, and by additivity of `Fr`,

> `Σ_m c_m Fr(m') = Σ_m Fr(c_m^{1/q} m') = Fr\big(Σ_m c_m^{1/q} m'\big) =: Fr(δ_ρ)`.

**Uniqueness.** The `ρ` are distinct `q`-reduced monomials, the factorisation of each monomial is
unique, and `Fr` is injective; so the `δ_ρ` are determined. ∎

**Remark B1 (why the old hypothesis was wrong, and load-bearing).** Versions `v11`–`v12` of the
assembly draft justified this expansion by *"`c^q = c` on `F_3`"*. That is a **special case**, and it is
**incompatible with the draft's own standing field** `K = \bar F_3` declared in §1, where `c^q = c`
holds only on the subfield `F_q`. Read literally, `v12` therefore needed `K = F_3` in §7.2′ and an
infinite `K` in §5 (Proposition 2.3) — two incompatible readings of the same chain. Perfectness
repairs both at once: `F_3`, `F_q` and `\bar F_3` are all perfect. **Nothing downstream changes**, since
the conclusion of 7.2′/7.2″ is unaltered.

**Remark B2.** Perfectness is exactly what is needed, not merely sufficient: over an imperfect field of
characteristic `3` (e.g. `F_3(t)`) the coefficient `t` has no `q`-th root and the expansion fails.

## 3 · Proof of (C) — where the frame lives, and where it does not

**C1 — the ceiling.** Draft Theorem 5.3 fixes a **linear** system of parameters `ℓ_1,…,ℓ_{k+1}` for `R`.
Over `K = \bar F_3` such a system exists unconditionally: `K` is infinite, so prime avoidance produces
the forms one at a time; `R` is a complete intersection, hence Cohen–Macaulay, hence free over
`C = K[ℓ_1,…,ℓ_{k+1}]` of rank `\deg R = (2k+1)!!` with basis degrees `g(z)`. **No genericity threshold
and no field-size condition enter** — the only hypothesis is that `K` is infinite. The carrying standalone
`CEILING_CI_STRUCTURE_THEOREM_v1` states this in its own scope section: *"for every `k ≥ 1` and every
`q = 3^v`, over `K = \bar F_3`, with no threshold and no frame hypothesis"*, and its item 4 records
explicitly that a **separating (`SEP`) frame over a finite field is a genuinely different and stronger
hypothesis**, which that document does not use.

**C2 — the conclusion returns to the prime field.** What Theorem 5.3 and Corollary 5.6 deliver is a
relocation of `\dim_K A_d`. By (A) that number is the same over `F_3` as over `\bar F_3`. **So the
linear system of parameters is a scaffold: it is erected over `\bar F_3`, used, and removed, and the
statement it proves never mentions it.**

**C3 — the collar, the window and the Ledger.** By Corollary A1 all four zone terms and `P_k(q)` are
field-independent integers, so C2 applies verbatim to the Ledger identity. Independently, the sweep of
§4 finds **no** frame-type hypothesis in any of the carrying standalones.

**C4 — what the chain does carry.** Stated once, so that nothing hides:
`char = 3` throughout; `char ≠ 2` where the swap count is used (draft Theorems 8.2, 8.5);
`K` perfect (7.2′, 7.2″, by (B)); `K` infinite (Proposition 2.3, supplied by §1).
**None of these four is a frame, and none is a threshold in `q`.**
