# CHAISE LONGUE — LEMMA (L): THE BALANCE-POINT CANONICAL LADDER
### `CHAISE_LONGUE_LEMMA_L_BALANCE_POINT_v1`  ·  standalone  ·  **PROVED ∀k**

**Statement (L).** Let `S≠T` be admissible prefix supports of `P_k` (each of size `k−1`,
`S={s_1<…<s_{k−1}}⊆{2,…,2k−1}`, `s_i≥2i`), with prefix generators
`g_R=∏_i x_{r_i}^{\,r_i−2(i−1)}`. Then `lcm(g_S,g_T) ∈ J'_{k−1}`, where `J'_{k−1}` is generated by
the admissible level-`p` monomials (`1≤p≤k−1`): support `U={u_1<…<u_{p+1}}`, cap `u_p=2p`,
`u_{p+1}=2p+1`, ballot `u_j≥2j`, exponents `u_j−2(j−1)`.

**Grade: PROVED ∀k** (pencil below; verified `k≤7`, 91 806 pairs at `k=7`, zero escapes — engine
`L_proof.py`).

---

## Two facts (each PROVED ∀k)

Write `M:=lcm(g_S,g_T)`, `U':=S∪T` sorted `u'_1<…<u'_R` (`R=|U'|≥k` since `S≠T`, both size `k−1`).

**Exponent parity.** For a support `R` and `v=r_i`, `exp(v)=r_i−2(i−1)`: even `v`⇒`exp≥2`, odd `v`⇒`exp≥3`.
Hence for a pair `{2p,2p+1}`: `x_{2p}^2 x_{2p+1} | M ⟺ {2p,2p+1}⊆U'`.

**Rank-monotonicity (foco 2).** `ρ(v):=` min rank of `v` in `S,T`. Then `ρ(u'_l)≤l` for all `l`
(the rank of `u'_l` in `S` is `#{s∈S:s≤u'_l}≤l`, same for `T`). A level support `W` divides `M`
iff `ρ(w_j)≤rank_W(w_j)` for every `j` (from exponent parity: `exp_W(w_j)=w_j−2(j−1)≤
w_j−2(ρ(w_j)−1)=exp_M(w_j)`).

---

## The balance point and the ladder `W(S,T)`

Let pairs `P_l={2l,2l+1}` (`l=1..k−1`) partition `{2,…,2k−1}`; `c_l=|U'∩P_l|`;
`N(p)=Σ_{l≤p}c_l=#{u'∈U':u'≤2p+1}`; `g(p)=N(p)−p`.

`g(0)=0`, `g(k−1)=|U'|−(k−1)≥1`, steps `g(p)−g(p−1)=c_p−1∈{−1,0,1}`.

**Define `p := min\{p≥1 : g(p)=1\}`** (the *balance point*). It exists and `1≤p≤k−1` (`g` first
reaches `1` no later than `k−1`). Since `g` moves by `±1/0` from `0`, the first hit of `1` is a `+1`
step, so `g(p−1)=0` and **`c_p=2`**, i.e. `{2p,2p+1}⊆U'`. And `N(p)=p+1`.

**`W := \{u'_1,…,u'_{p+1}\}`** = the `p+1` smallest elements of `U'` (exactly those `≤2p+1`, since
`N(p)=p+1`). Its top two are `2p,2p+1` (the two largest `≤2p+1`), so the cap is `{2p,2p+1}`.

**(a) `W` is an admissible level-`p` support.** For `j<p`, `g(j)≤0` (minimality of `p`), so
`N(j)≤j`, i.e. at most `j` elements are `≤2j+1`, forcing `u'_{j+1}≥2(j+1)`. Thus `w_i≥2i` for
`i=1..p`; the cap gives `w_p=2p`, `w_{p+1}=2p+1`. Ballot holds. ✓

**(b) `gen(W) | M`.** `w_j=u'_j`, so `ρ(w_j)=ρ(u'_j)≤j` by rank-monotonicity — exactly the divisibility
condition. ✓

**(c) `p≤k−1`**, so `gen(W)` is a genuine generator of `J'_{k−1}`.

Therefore `gen(W) | M`, i.e. `M=lcm(g_S,g_T)∈J'_{k−1}`. **∎**

*Worked example* (`k=6`): `S=(4,5,6,10,11,13)`, `T=(5,6,8,9,10,13)`, `U'={4,5,6,8,9,10,11,13}`.
`g:` `g(1)=−1,g(2)=0,g(3)=0,g(4)=1`→ balance point `p=4`, cap `{8,9}`, `W=(4,5,6,8,9)` =
`x_4^4x_5^3x_6^2x_8^2x_9 ∈ J'_5`, divides `lcm`. ✓

## Immediate corollaries

**Unique prefix divisor (∀k).** If `μ∈P_k\J'_{k−1}` were divisible by `g_S` and `g_T` (`S≠T`), then
`lcm(g_S,g_T)|μ`, and by (L) `lcm∈J'_{k−1}`, so `μ∈J'_{k−1}` — contradiction. So every
`μ∈(P_k+J'_{k−1})\J'_{k−1}` has a **unique** prefix divisor `g_{S(μ)}`; the clack `φ(μ)=μ/∏_{s∈S(μ)}x_s²`
is well-defined `∀k`.

**Kills (dead ends, cementerio).** The greedy ladder "smallest full pair + one element per lower pair"
(`v113`) fails when a lower pair is empty (`S=(4,5,6),T=(4,5,7)` skips `{2,3}`). The balance point
sidesteps this: it never fixes the cap by pigeonhole alone; it reads the first index where the
prefix-sum crosses the diagonal.

**Grade: (L) PROVED ∀k; unique divisor PROVED ∀k.** Does not by itself close `(*)`, GAP 3, or (B).
