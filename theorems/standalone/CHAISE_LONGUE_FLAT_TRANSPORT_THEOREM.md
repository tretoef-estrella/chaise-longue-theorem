# The Flat-Transport Theorem

### Local sealing of the odd-elementary residue complex is dimension-independent: it transports from a fixed model stratum to every concurrency flat, in every dimension

**Chaise Longue campaign · standalone · v1 · Marsh (Auditor), from Frescales VIII turn 5**

---

## Abstract

Let `K` be a field of odd characteristic `p`, `q=p^v`, and for each `k≥1` let `R_k = S_k/E_k` be the quotient of
`S_k=K[x_1,\dots,x_{2k+2}]` by the ideal `E_k=(e_1,e_3,\dots,e_{2k+1})` generated by the odd elementary symmetric
polynomials. The contraction problem `(\star)_k:\ \mathfrak m^{[q]}_{R_k}=R_k\cap \mathfrak m^{[q]}_{\overline
R_k}` localizes, after the mirror-parity reduction, to the vanishing of the homology of a **residue complex**
`\Phi_F` at each concurrency flat `F` of the sheet arrangement. We prove that `\Phi_F`, for a flat of a fixed
combinatorial type, is the flat base change of a single **model complex** `\Phi_B` — independent of `k` — by the
polynomial ring in the spectator coordinates, modulo a subcomplex supported on strictly deeper flats.
Consequently homology transports:
```
    H^{i}(\Phi_F) \;\cong\; H^{i}(\Phi_B)\otimes_K K[u_{\mathrm{spect}}] \pmod{\text{deeper strata}},
```
so **the sealing of the finitely many model complexes forces the local sealing of every concurrency flat, in
every dimension `k`.** The theorem is a transport principle: it converts an infinite family of local vanishing
statements into finitely many, isolating the residual content of the conjecture in the model strata alone.

---

## 1. Standing objects

Fix `K`, `\mathrm{char}\,K=p` odd, `q=p^v`, `n=2k+2`. Write `S=K[x_1,\dots,x_n]`,
`E=(e_1,e_3,\dots,e_{2k+1})`, `R=S/E`. The following are established in the campaign and used as input:

- **(CI)** `E` is a reduced complete intersection of dimension `k+1`; its minimal primes are
  `I_J=(x_{a}+x_{b}:\{a,b\}\in J)` over the perfect matchings `J` of `\{1,\dots,n\}`, with `N=(2k+1)!!` sheets
  `L_J=V(I_J)`; on `L_J` the matched pair `\{a_l,b_l\}` carries `x_{a_l}=u_l,\ x_{b_l}=-u_l` (sign-opposite).
- **(Loc)** After the mirror-parity split, the obstruction to `(\star)` is supported on the codimension-`\ge 2`
  **concurrency flats** of the arrangements `\{u_i=\pm u_l\}`; codimension one seals unconditionally.
- **(A′)** *(Corollary A′, the slice base change.)* At a concurrency flat `F` whose active block `B` consists of
  `2j` collapsed coordinates, the coordinates split into the **active block** `B` (values `\pm s` on the
  collapse locus) and **spectator pairs** (distinct generic values `\pm t_m`). The `2j`-coordinate active
  arrangement is a copy of the smaller problem; the spectator pairs are common to all sheets through `F`; and
  the conductor factors as `Q_J = Q_{\mathrm{act}}\cdot D`, where `D` is a nonzerodivisor common to those
  sheets whose vanishing locus `V(D)` is the union of the strictly deeper concurrency flats meeting `F`.

We work throughout with **finite-length** modules (`\mathfrak m^{[q]}` is `\mathfrak m`-primary; all residue
data is supported scheme-theoretically at the origin) and therefore at the level of the **Čech / seam complex
of the sheet cover** — never by localizing the coordinate ring at the prime of a positive-dimensional flat,
which would invert a spectator `x_m^q` and trivialize the complex.

## 2. The residue complex and the model complex

For a concurrency flat `F` with sheet cover `\{L_\sigma\}_{\sigma\in\Sigma_F}` (the sheets through `F`), let
`\Phi_F` denote the **residue complex**: in degree `0`, the per-sheet residue data `(\rho_{\cdot,\sigma},
w_{\cdot,\sigma})` subject to the pinning `\sum_l u_l^{q}\rho_{l,\sigma}=z|_{L_\sigma}`; in degree `1`, the seam
agreement conditions (odd charge, odd shared, even knob-absorption, glue) over the seams of `F`; with the
seam-discrepancy differential. `H^{1}(\Phi_F)=0` per graded degree is exactly the statement that every
per-sheet-consistent residue `z` glues — the **local sealing** at `F`.

Let `B` be the combinatorial type of the active block of `F` (equivalently, the isomorphism type of the active
matching sub-arrangement, with its seam nerve `\mathrm{Cay}(S_j,\text{transp})`). Let `\Phi_B` be the residue
complex of the **model stratum** of type `B`: the same construction carried out for the `2j`-coordinate active
arrangement alone, over `K[v_1,\dots,v_j]/(v^{q})` on each of its `|S_j|` sheets. `\Phi_B` depends only on `B`
(equivalently on `j`), **not on `k`.** Write `K[u_{\mathrm{spect}}]` for the polynomial ring in the spectator
coordinates of `F` (there are `2(k-j)` of them at a level-`k` flat of type `B`).

## 3. The theorem

> **Theorem (Flat Transport).** Fix a block type `B`. For every `k\ge j` and every concurrency flat `F` of type
> `B` in the level-`k` arrangement, there is an isomorphism of complexes of graded `K`-modules
> ```
>     \Phi_F \;\cong\; \Phi_B \otimes_K K[u_{\mathrm{spect}}] \qquad\text{in }\ \mathcal D_F,
> ```
> where `\mathcal D_F` is the quotient of the category of `\Phi`-type complexes by those supported on strictly
> deeper flats (i.e. the isomorphism holds modulo `\Phi`-subcomplexes supported on `V(D)`). Consequently, per
> graded degree and for every `i`,
> ```
>     H^{i}(\Phi_F) \;\cong\; H^{i}(\Phi_B)\otimes_K K[u_{\mathrm{spect}}] \qquad (\bmod\ \text{deeper strata}).
> ```
> In particular, if `H^{i}(\Phi_B)=0` then `H^{i}(\Phi_F)=0` modulo deeper strata, for every `k\ge j`.

### Proof

**Step 1 (the split, from A′).** By **(A′)**, on the sheets through `F` the coordinate ring is
`K[v_1,\dots,v_j]\otimes_K K[u_{\mathrm{spect}}]` with the active variables `v` carrying the model arrangement
and the spectator variables `u_{\mathrm{spect}}` common to all sheets of `F`. Each structure map of `\Phi_F`
— the pinning `\sum u_l^{q}\rho_{l}=z`, the odd charge and shared conditions, the even knob-absorptions and
glue — involves a spectator channel only through its pinning coefficient `u_m^{q}` and, on the conductor, the
common factor `D`. Extracting the common spectator data (Frobenius-kill: `u_m^{q}\equiv 0` on each seam mod
`\mathfrak m^{[q]}`; and the mixed-Koszul gauge, which makes the spectator data common across the sheets of `F`
at zero cost to the active data) exhibits an isomorphism of complexes
```
    \Phi_F \;\cong\; \Phi_B \otimes_K K[u_{\mathrm{spect}}] \;\oplus\; (\text{terms in } \operatorname{coker}(\cdot D)),
```
where `\Phi_B` is the active model complex and `\operatorname{coker}(\cdot D)` is supported on `V(D)`. By
**(A′)**, `V(D)` is the union of the strictly deeper concurrency flats meeting `F`; hence the second summand
vanishes in `\mathcal D_F`, giving `\Phi_F\cong \Phi_B\otimes_K K[u_{\mathrm{spect}}]` in `\mathcal D_F`.

**Step 2 (flat base change commutes with homology).** `K[u_{\mathrm{spect}}]` is a free `K`-module (basis: the
monomials in the spectator variables), hence flat. For any complex `C^{\bullet}` of `K`-modules and any flat
`K`-module `M`, the functor `-\otimes_K M` is exact, so it commutes with the formation of homology:
`H^{i}(C^{\bullet}\otimes_K M)\cong H^{i}(C^{\bullet})\otimes_K M`. Applying this with `C^{\bullet}=\Phi_B`,
`M=K[u_{\mathrm{spect}}]`, in each graded degree (where all modules are finite-dimensional over `K`), yields
```
    H^{i}\!\big(\Phi_B\otimes_K K[u_{\mathrm{spect}}]\big)\;\cong\;H^{i}(\Phi_B)\otimes_K K[u_{\mathrm{spect}}].
```

**Step 3 (combine).** Homology is a functor on `\mathcal D_F`, so Step 1 gives
`H^{i}(\Phi_F)\cong H^{i}(\Phi_B\otimes_K K[u_{\mathrm{spect}}])` modulo deeper strata, and Step 2 identifies the
right-hand side with `H^{i}(\Phi_B)\otimes_K K[u_{\mathrm{spect}}]`. If `H^{i}(\Phi_B)=0`, the right-hand side is
`0`, hence `H^{i}(\Phi_F)=0` modulo deeper strata. `\qquad\blacksquare`

### Remark (why "modulo deeper strata" is exactly right, not a weakness)

The relative form is dictated by the finite-length geometry: no absolute (localized) statement is available,
because localizing at the prime of a positive-dimensional flat inverts a spectator `x_m^{q}` and kills every
module. The deeper-strata subcomplex is precisely `\operatorname{coker}(\cdot D)`, and it is disposed of by the
**deepest-first (flasque/Cousin) assembly** `(\mathrm A\star)` already established for the flat poset: ordering
the concurrency flats by depth and discharging them innermost-first, each `\Phi_F` is treated after all strictly
deeper `\Phi_{F'}` have been shown to seal, so the quotient in `\mathcal D_F` is exactly the object that the
induction consumes. This is the sense in which the transport is complete.

## 4. Corollaries

> **Corollary 1 (finite reduction of local sealing).** If `H^{>0}(\Phi_B)=0` for each block type `B` occurring
> in the arrangements (equivalently, for the generating model strata under the depth recursion), then the
> residue complex seals locally at every concurrency flat, for every `k`.

*Proof.* Immediate from the Theorem and the deepest-first assembly `(\mathrm A\star)`: the base of the induction
is the innermost model stratum, and each successive flat seals by transport from its model, modulo the deeper
flats already discharged. `\blacksquare`

> **Corollary 2 (dimension independence).** The truth value of "the residue complex seals locally at every
> concurrency flat of type `B`" is independent of `k`: it is determined by the single model complex `\Phi_B`.

*Proof.* Both directions of the equivalence `H^{>0}(\Phi_B)=0\iff H^{>0}(\Phi_F)=0\ (\bmod\text{ deeper})` are
given by the Theorem (flat base change is faithfully flat when `K[u_{\mathrm{spect}}]\ne 0`, so the tensor
vanishes iff the factor `H^{i}(\Phi_B)` does). `\blacksquare`

## 5. Scope — what the theorem gives and what it does not

- **Gives.** A reduction of an infinite family of *local* vanishing statements (one per concurrency flat per
  `k`) to finitely many model computations. The `k`-dependence of the local homology is confined entirely to
  the free spectator tensor factor; it carries no new geometry.
- **Does not give — (i) the base sealing.** The Theorem is a *transport* principle. Whether the model complexes
  `\Phi_B` themselves seal (`H^{>0}(\Phi_B)=0`) is the input, established separately: for the triple-flat model
  it is the `k=2` computation, where the odd (`\rho`) coupling and a genuinely characteristic-`p` gauge
  phenomenon at the Frobenius degree enter. The present theorem says only that *if* the models seal, all levels
  seal locally.
- **Does not give — (ii) the global class.** Local sealing at every concurrency flat does not by itself close
  `(\star)`: the descent of the pair-fixing cover leaves a global class `[\theta_k]\in H^{1}(K_{2k+1};\mathcal
  G)` on the partner graph, disposed of separately by the Clebsch–Gordan gate. The Flat-Transport Theorem
  supplies the local input `\mathcal G` for that gate, uniformly in `k`; it does not evaluate the class.

## 7. One line

Once the residue complex is written at a concurrency flat, its `k`-dependence is a free polynomial factor in the
spectator coordinates; homology ignores that factor, so local sealing is a property of finitely many model
strata and transports verbatim to every dimension — the infinite family of local vanishing statements collapses
to a finite check, and the whole residual content of the conjecture is pushed into the model strata and the
global partner-graph class.
