## 0. The problem, named exactly

SLAP 4 (Theorem 4A) proves α_c(k,q) = 0 for c < min(q, k(k+1)) — the onset of the annihilator — **for (k,q) admitting a SEP transverse frame over F_q**, guaranteed only for q ≥ q₀(k) := D(k)+1 with D(k) ≤ (2k+1)!!·(k+2)³ (Lemma 4.3), an astronomical threshold; and F₃-frames genuinely fail to exist in general (Hammock H6: no F₃ frame at k=3). Theorem M (A_d = ᾱ_{σ−d}) carried the same frame tag in the Dimension Ledger. This is the audit finding P0-7 (FRESH_EYES_AUDIT v2 §3): an untraced scope condition contaminating links 3–5 of the Main Theorem A chain.

The coupling has a single source. SLAP 4 §0 reads: *"For a^q = a we take frame coefficients in F_q (Frobenius linearity intact for THIS q)."* The present document removes that hypothesis and shows the entire mechanism runs with frame coefficients in an arbitrary extension field K ⊇ F₃, with all forced constants Frobenius-twisted — twisting that is harmless because Frobenius is a field automorphism of K. The frame field and the Frobenius level q are independent parameters. Consequences: one fixed frame over one fixed finite field K(k) serves **every level of the tower simultaneously**, and the vanishing, the confinement, and the mirror hold for all k and all q with no frame threshold.

## 1. Setting

S = F₃[x₁,…,x_n], n = 2k+2. E = (e₁, e₃, …, e_{2k+1}). Sheets V_J (J a perfect matching of [n]), linear ideals I_J, pair coordinates w_p on V_J. For a field K ⊇ F₃, write S_K = K ⊗ S, and for an F₃-defined graded ideal I ⊆ S write I_K ⊆ S_K for its extension. The A-ring is S/(E + m^{[q]}); A_d(k,q) := dim_{F₃}(S/(E+m^{[q]}))_d.

A **transverse frame over K** is a tuple ℓ₁,…,ℓ_{k+1} ∈ (S_K)₁ with every sheet matrix B_J = (coords of ℓ|_{V_J}) invertible, together with g₁,…,g_k ∈ (S_K)₁ completing (e₁, ℓ₁,…,ℓ_{k+1}) to a basis of (S_K)₁. **SEP** is SLAP 4's separation condition (Definition, §2 there): for every swap-adjacent pair (J,J′), some g_i restricts on V_J ∪ V_{J′} outside span_K(ℓ|). Λ_K := S_K/(E + (ℓ₁^q,…,ℓ_{k+1}^q)); by the Sandwich Teorema 3 and Bridge (s21–s22, run over K), Λ_K is a graded Artinian complete intersection of degrees 1,3,…,2k+1,q,…,q (hence Gorenstein) with socle degree σ = (k+1)(q+k−1), and ker(Λ_K ↠ K ⊗ A-ring) = (g₁^q,…,g_k^q)·Λ_K. α_c(k,q;K,frame) := dim_K ann_{Λ_K}(g₁^q,…,g_k^q)_c.

## 2. Lemma FD1 (Rank Descent)

**Lemma FD1.** Let I ⊆ S be generated by F₃-defined forms. For every field K ⊇ F₃ and every d: dim_K (S_K/I_K)_d = dim_{F₃}(S/I)_d. In particular A_d(k,q) is invariant under base change, and any vanishing or equality of such dimensions proved over K holds over F₃.

*Proof.* (S/I)_d is the cokernel of a matrix M_d with entries in F₃ (coefficients of the generators times monomials). Row reduction of M_d over F₃ is a valid row reduction over K with the same pivots; rank_K(M_d) = rank_{F₃}(M_d). ∎

## 3. Lemma FD2 (Twisted Witness — the hypothesis a^q = a removed)

**Lemma FD2.** Let K ⊇ F₃ be any field, and let (ℓ, g) be a transverse frame over K. Then SLAP 4's Lemma 4.1 holds verbatim with Frobenius-twisted constants: for c < q and x ∈ (C_q)_c with global witnesses y_{ij},
> y_{ij}|_{V_J} = c̃^J_{ij} · x|_{V_J}, where c̃^J = solution of the twisted linear system with matrix B_J^{(q)} := (entrywise q-th powers of B_J),
and Lemma 4.2 (agreement on swap flats) holds with the c̃'s. Moreover the SEP condition is unchanged: c̃^J = c̃^{J′} for all (i,j) if and only if c^J = c^{J′} for all (i,j) — separation over K is the same Zariski-open condition as in SLAP 4.

*Proof.* Frobenius φ_q: a ↦ a^q is a ring endomorphism of any ring of characteristic 3 with q = 3^v, so for ℓ_j|_{V_J} = Σ_p b_{jp} w_p with b_{jp} ∈ K we get ℓ_j^q|_{V_J} = Σ_p b_{jp}^q w_p^q — the restriction of a q-th power is a K-combination of the w_p^q with **twisted** coefficients. The degree decoupling of SLAP 4's proof (c + q < 2q ⟹ each monomial has exponent ≥ q in exactly one pair slot; disjoint supports) is pure degree arithmetic, field-free. Reading off the w_p^q-coefficients gives, exactly as there, φ_q(coords of g_i)·x̄ = (B_J^{(q)})ᵀ·ȳ_i. On a **perfect** field K (any finite field or F̄₃), φ_q is a field automorphism, so B_J^{(q)} = φ_q(B_J) entrywise satisfies det(B_J^{(q)}) = φ_q(det B_J) ≠ 0: the twisted system is uniquely solvable and yields the display with c̃^J = (B_J^{(q)})^{-T}·φ_q(coords of g_i). Lemma 4.2 is unchanged (one global y restricted from two sheets). For the last claim: c̃^J = φ_q-image of the untwisted solution data, and φ_q is injective, so equality of the twisted tuples across a swap pair is equivalent to equality of the untwisted ones; the untwisted equality is exactly "g_i − Σ c_{ij}ℓ_j vanishes on both sheets," i.e. the negation of SEP, as in SLAP 4. ∎

## 4. Lemma FD3 (One frame for the whole tower)

**Lemma FD3.** Fix k. There is a finite field K(k) = F_{3^M}, with any M such that 3^M > D(k) (D(k) the explicit bound of SLAP 4 Lemma 4.3), and a single transverse SEP frame (ℓ, g) over K(k), **independent of q**, which is transverse and SEP simultaneously for every level q = 3^v of the tower. Over F̄₃ such frames exist for every k trivially.

*Proof.* Transversality (all B_J invertible) and SEP are conditions on the linear forms (ℓ, g) against the fixed sheets V_J and their swap pairs; **neither condition mentions q**. (In SLAP 4 the q entered only through the frame-field choice, removed by FD2.) Both are finite conjunctions of polynomial non-vanishing conditions on the coefficients, jointly nonempty-open by SLAP 4 Lemma 4.3's dimension count (unchanged). A nonempty open subset of affine coefficient space defined by conditions of total degree ≤ D(k) has a point over any field with more than D(k) elements by Schwartz–Zippel — in particular over F_{3^M} with 3^M > D(k). ∎

**Corollary FD-C (the stable colon, tower-uniform).** With the fixed frame of FD3, the colon object C°(k) of SLAP 1/3 is **literally one fixed K(k)-ideal across the entire tower** (the open condition of SLAP 4 §4.2, previously wanted as "F₃-frames ∀k," is delivered over K(k) instead), and its graded census is q-free in the ranges already proven (Twin R8), with all dimensions descending to F₃ by FD1.

## 5. Theorem FD-A (Vanishing and confinement, no frame threshold)

> **Theorem FD-A.** For every k ≥ 1 and every q = 3^v:
> **α_c(k,q) = 0 for all c < min(q, k(k+1)),**
> where α is computed in Λ_K for any transverse SEP frame over any perfect K ⊇ F₃ (exists for every (k,q) by FD3). Consequently, by the Gorenstein mirror of Λ_K (socle σ = (k+1)(q+k−1)):
> **A_d(k,q) = 0 for all d > (k+1)(q−1), for every k and every q > k(k+1),**
> with the F₃ statement obtained from the K statement by Lemma FD1. No condition on frames over F_q or F₃ remains; q₀(k) does not appear.

*Proof.* Run SLAP 4's Theorem 4A over K with FD2 in place of Lemma 4.1: the twisted constants satisfy the same agreement (Lemma 4.2) and the same separation dichotomy (FD2, last claim), so for each of the k(k+1) swap neighbours of a fixed sheet J the class x vanishes identically on the corresponding swap hyperplane of V_J — this is a ring-level identity in O(V_F) ((c̃^J − c̃^{J′})·x|_{V_F} = 0 with a nonzero scalar factor), not a rational-point statement, so no density issue arises over any field. O(V_J) = K[w] is a UFD; the k(k+1) swap forms are pairwise non-proportional (char 3); their product divides x|_{V_J}; degree count kills x on every sheet; Radicality (an F₃-defined ideal identity, valid over K by flat base change) gives x ∈ E_K. The confinement corollary is SLAP 4's, over K; A_d descends by FD1. ∎

## 6. Theorem FD-M (The mirror, unconditional, and the frame-independence of ᾱ)

> **Theorem FD-M.** For every k and every q = 3^v, with any transverse frame over any K as in FD3:
> **A_d(k,q) = ᾱ_{σ−d}(k,q) for all d, σ = (k+1)(q+k−1),**
> where ᾱ_c := dim ann_{Λ_K}(g₁^q,…,g_k^q)_c. In particular ᾱ_c is independent of the frame and of K, being equal to A_{σ−c}(k,q), an F₃-invariant. Theorem M's Ledger tag "scope frame (peaje E intacto)" is discharged for the frame half: the mirror holds at every (k,q); what remains of the peaje E is only the small-q census content, untouched.

*Proof.* Λ_K is a graded Artinian complete intersection (§1), hence Gorenstein with one-dimensional socle in degree σ = Σ(dᵢ−1) = (0+2+⋯+2k) + (k+1)(q−1) = (k+1)(q+k−1). For a graded ideal I ⊆ Λ_K of an Artinian Gorenstein ring, the perfect pairing Λ_K,c × Λ_K,σ−c → Λ_K,σ ≅ K identifies ann(I)_c with the annihilator of I under the pairing, giving dim ann(I)_c = dim (Λ_K/I)_{σ−c} (Matlis duality for graded Artinian Gorenstein rings — standard). Take I = (g₁^q,…,g_k^q)·Λ_K: by the Bridge over K (§1), Λ_K/I = K ⊗ A-ring, whose graded dimensions are A_{σ−c}(k,q) by FD1. ∎
