## Theorem LB (the lower half of (C1), ∀k) — **PROVED**

> **`g₁ − g₂ ∉ J`; the constant relation space `R_const = {c ∈ F₃^{2k} : Σc_αg_α ∈ J}`
> equals `⟨(1,…,1)⟩` exactly; hence `dim span{g_α mod J} = 2k−1` and**
> ## `dim K_{2k−1} ≥ 2k−1 for every k.`

*Proof.* Suppose `g₁ − g₂ ∈ J`. By F3, `(g₁−g₂)|_ρ = 2v·ū′ = −v·ū′`, which must lie in the
ideal generated by `ρ(J)` in `S′[v]` — by HINGE H2–H4 that ideal is generated by
`{f′_{2j+1} − v²f′_{2j−1}}_{j≤k−2}`, `e₁′ū′ + v²f′_{2k−3}`, `(e₁′²+v²)ū′`. Reduce the
membership modulo `F′ := (f′₃,…,f′_{2k−3})·S′[v]`: it becomes
`ū′·[v + Be₁′ + C(e₁′²+v²)] ∈ F′` for some `B, C ∈ S′[v]`. By Theorem S at level k−1,
`(f′₃,…,f′_{2k−3}, ū′)` is a regular sequence, so `ū′` is a nonzerodivisor mod `F′` (colon
commutes with the flat extension `S′[v]`), giving `v + Be₁′ + C(e₁′²+v²) ∈ F′`. Its
`v¹`-coefficient is `1 + B₁e₁′ + C₁e₁′² ∈ (f′₃,…,f′_{2k−3}) ⊆ m′` — but it has constant
term 1. Contradiction. So `g₁ − g₂ ∉ J`, and by `S_{2k}`-equivariance no transposition
difference lies in J. `R_const` is an `S_{2k}`-submodule of the permutation module
`F₃^{2k}`, whose full submodule lattice is `0 ⊂ ⟨1⟩ ⊂ Z (zero-sum) ⊂ F₃^{2k}` when
`3 | 2k` and `{0, ⟨1⟩, Z, F₃^{2k}}` when `3 ∤ 2k` (Z irreducible resp. Z/⟨1⟩ irreducible).
`R_const ⊇ ⟨1⟩` (Theorem R(b)) and `(1,−1,0,…,0) ∈ Z ∖ R_const`, so in both cases
`R_const = ⟨1⟩`. ∎
*(Gates: g₁−g₂ ∉ J and `R_const` solved exactly — dim 1, basis (1,…,1) — at k = 2, 3, 4.)*
