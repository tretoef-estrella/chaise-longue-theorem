# THE ODD SYMMETRIC GENERATION THEOREM, AND THE ODD AXIS
## `E = (e_1, e_3, …, e_{2k+1})` in every characteristic ≠ 2 — and `A_k(q) = P_k(q)` for every **odd** `q`, in characteristic 0 as well
### Chaise Longue campaign — standalone deposit v1
**Author:** Orfila (auditor) · **Origin of the question:** Lacassagne (odd-axis measurement, turn 1) · **Machine:** Rafa · 27 Jul 2026

---

## ABSTRACT

Let `n = 2k+2`, `S = K[x_1,…,x_n]`, `char K ≠ 2`. Let `L_J` be the `(2k+1)!!` matching sheets
(`x_a + x_b = 0` along the edges of a perfect matching `J` of `[n]`) and `E = ⋂_J I_J`.

> ### **THEOREM 1 (generation).** `E = (e_1, e_3, e_5, …, e_{2k+1})`, the complete intersection
> ### generated by the **elementary symmetric polynomials of odd index**. Valid for every `k` and
> ### every field of characteristic `≠ 2`.

> ### **MEASUREMENT (the odd axis).** With `A_k(q) := dim_K S/(E + (x_1^q,…,x_n^q))`:
> ### **`A_k(q) = P_k(q)` for every ODD `q` tested, in characteristic 0 as well as in characteristic 3.**
> ### 14 cells, `k ≤ 5`. Even-`q` controls fail, as they must.

Theorem 1 is what makes the measurement possible at all: it replaces the intersection of `105`
(at `k=3`) or `945` (at `k=4`) ideals by `k+1` explicit generators.

> ### **⚠️ The odd axis is MEASURED, not proved, in any cell. The conjecture remains open.**

---

## 1 · THEOREM 1 AND ITS PROOF

**THEOREM 1.** For `char K ≠ 2`, `E = (e_1, e_3, …, e_{2k+1})`.

**Proof.** Over `K̄`, a point `x = (x_1,…,x_n)` lies on some sheet `L_J` if and only if its multiset
of coordinates can be partitioned into pairs `{a, −a}`, i.e. **the multiset is closed under
negation**. That holds if and only if `∏_{i=1}^{n}(T − x_i)` is an **even** polynomial in `T`, i.e.
all coefficients of odd powers of `T` vanish. Those coefficients are, up to sign, exactly
`e_1, e_3, …, e_{2k+1}` (note `n = 2k+2` is even, so the odd-index elementary symmetric polynomials
run precisely to `e_{2k+1}`). Hence

>   `V(e_1, e_3, …, e_{2k+1}) = ⋃_J L_J`   set-theoretically.

Write `A = (e_1, e_3, …, e_{2k+1})`. It is generated by `k+1` forms in `n = 2k+2` variables and its
zero locus has dimension `k+1`, so `codim A = k+1` equals the number of generators: `A` is a
**complete intersection**, hence unmixed and Cohen–Macaulay, and by Bézout

>   `deg A = 1·3·5⋯(2k+1) = (2k+1)!!`.

The union `⋃_J L_J` has exactly `(2k+1)!!` irreducible components, each a linear subspace of degree
`1`, so `deg(⋃_J L_J) = (2k+1)!!` as well. An unmixed ideal whose degree equals the degree of its
(reduced) zero locus is radical. Therefore `A = I(⋃_J L_J) = ⋂_J I_J = E`. ∎

**Corollary.** `S/E` is a complete intersection of degrees `1, 3, …, 2k+1` **with explicit
generators**, in every characteristic `≠ 2`. This is the presentation the campaign had been using
abstractly (via its Hilbert series) without generators in hand.

**Remark — characteristic 2.** For `char K = 2` the sheets `x_a + x_b = 0` and `x_a − x_b = 0`
coincide and the argument collapses; the hypothesis is essential, as it is throughout the campaign.

## 2 · THE TRAP THIS REPLACES — power sums degenerate

The natural first candidate is the odd **power sums** `p_1, p_3, …, p_{2k+1}` (the multiset is closed
under negation iff all odd power sums vanish — true over `Q`). **In characteristic 3 this fails:**
by Frobenius `p_3 = p_1^3 ∈ (p_1)`, so the would-be complete intersection degenerates.

| cell | with `p_j`, char 3 | correct value |
|---|---|---|
| `k=1, q=3` | `27 = 3^3` | **19** |
| `k=2, q=3` | `243 = 3^5` | **141** |

In characteristic 0 the power sums do give the right values (`19, 61, 127, 141`). **The degeneration
produces no error message — it produces a plausible number.** The `e_j` do not degenerate.
Recorded as failure mode `POWER-SUMS-DEGENERATE-IN-CHAR-P`.

## 3 · THE ODD AXIS — measurement

Engine `CHAISE_LONGUE_ODD_AXIS.sing` (Singular), run on the Architect's machine. **Gates declared
before running**, and computed by a route that touches no Gröbner basis: `P_k(q)` for odd prime
powers is the number of vectors in `F_q^n` whose multiset of coordinates is closed under negation,
obtained by a multinomial count.

**Death criterion, pre-registered:** odd `q` must match in char 0 **and** char 3 — one miss kills the
claim; even `q` must **not** match — a match there would make the discriminator bogus and the claim
vacuous.

| `k` | `q` | gate `P_k(q)` | char 0 | char 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 5 | 18733 | ✓ | ✓ |
| 3 | 7 | 103279 | ✓ | ✓ |
| 3 | 9 | 345465 | ✓ | ✓ |
| 4 | 3 | 8953 | ✓ | ✓ |
| 4 | 5 | 375745 | ✓ | ✓ |
| 5 | 3 | 73789 | ✓ | ✓ |

**12/12.** Plus `k=1` (`q = 3,5,7`) and `k=2` (`q = 3,5`) reproduced independently by the auditor
in exact arithmetic, with characteristic 0 probed by **two distinct large primes** that agreed.

**Even controls (`k = 3`, char 3):** `q = 4` gives `4900` against `P_3(4) = 5650` (difference `750`);
`q = 6` gives `44730` against `P_3(6) = 47916` (difference `3186`). **Both fail, as required.**

**Cross-check obtained for free.** Interpolating `P_3` through the odd cells gives
`P_3(q) = 105q^4 − 630q^3 + 1645q^2 − 2037q + 918`: leading coefficient `105 = (2k+1)!!`, and second
coefficient `−630 = −deg C`. This **confirms the `SECOND_COEFFICIENT_THEOREM` at `k = 3` by a route
that touches no module at all** — interpolated point counts.

## 4 · WHAT THIS CHANGES

The conjecture was stated, and attacked for the whole campaign, as a statement about
**characteristic 3** and **`q = 3^v`**, with condition (I) of `GAP 3` reading *"does the Frobenius
power commute with the intersection of the sheets?"*. The measurement says the phenomenon is
**neither about characteristic 3 nor about powers of 3**: it is about `q` being **odd**, and it holds
verbatim in characteristic 0, where there is no Frobenius at all.

Combining with Theorem 1, the statement to be proved becomes

> ### `dim_K K[x_1,…,x_n] / (e_1, e_3, …, e_{2k+1}, x_1^q, …, x_n^q) = P_k(q)`, `q` odd, `char K ≠ 2`

— **a closed, characteristic-free statement about a complete intersection with explicit generators
plus a monomial ideal, with `q` a free integer parameter.** No Frobenius, no tower, no tight closure.
Induction on `q` and deformation in `q` become available; neither existed before.

## 5 · SCOPE — WHAT THIS DOES *NOT* CLAIM

1. **The odd axis is MEASURED in 14 cells and PROVED in none.** The conjecture is open, `GAP 3` is
   open, `G = 4`. Theorem 1 alone is proved.
2. **It does not close, narrow, or reframe any `GAP` of the assembly draft.** Nothing in the draft
   becomes false; the scope **widens**.
3. It says nothing about `q` even beyond the two control cells, and offers no explanation for why
   parity is the discriminator.
4. The campaign's proved method exclusions were established against the characteristic-3 object.
   **Their validity against the characteristic-free statement is NOT verified** and must be
   re-crossed before being invoked there. In particular the monomial/initial-ideal exclusion
   (*Impossibility of the Casing*) must be re-examined in the new setting rather than assumed.
5. Cells reported by the constructor beyond those listed here are **not** included: two self-caught
   bugs (a `vdim = −1` false confirmation, and a tautological gate) are pending audit. The 12 cells
   above come from an engine validated `8/8` at `k = 1, 2` before shipping, with gates independent of
   any Gröbner computation.
