## 1. The Z-Dressing Lemma (PROVED, ∀q = 3^v, polynomial identity)
In `R'[z]` (any coefficient ring, char 3), for any `x_b, x_{b'}`:
> ### `[z^{q−1}]\;\{(x_b − z)^{q−1}(x_{b'} + z)^{q−1}\} \;=\; s_{bb'}^{\,q−1}`, `s_{bb'} = x_b + x_{b'}`.
*Proof.* By Lucas, `(x_b−z)^{q−1} = Σ_j x_b^{q−1−j}z^j` and `(x_{b'}+z)^{q−1} = Σ_i(−1)^i x_{b'}^{q−1−i}z^i`. The `z^{q−1}` coefficient is `Σ_{i=0}^{q−1}(−x_b)^i x_{b'}^{q−1−i}`, the alternating complete sum, which telescopes against `(x_b + x_{b'})`: `(x_b+x_{b'})·Σ(−x_b)^ix_{b'}^{q−1−i} = x_{b'}^q − (−x_b)^q = x_{b'}^q + x_b^q = s_{bb'}^q` (Frobenius, char 3). Hence the coefficient equals `s_{bb'}^{q−1}` identically. ∎ *(Verified symbolically at `q = 3, 9, 27`: 3/3.)*
Full expansion, for the record: `[z^m]` of the dressed pair is the alternating sum `Σ_{i+j=m}(−1)^i x_b^{q−1−j}x_{b'}^{q−1−i}` — at `q=3`: `x_b²x_{b'}²`, `x_bx_{b'}(x_{b'}−x_b)`, `s_{bb'}²`.

**What it is:** when the level-`k` matching pairs `{b, 2k−1}` and `{b', 2k}` instead of `{2k−1,2k}`, the reduction mod `s = s_{2k−1,2k}` dresses the pair in `z`; the Lemma says the TOP `z`-slice of the dressed pair is EXACTLY the seam power `s_{bb'}^{q−1}` that the level-`(k−1)` generator needs. The broken seam re-solders itself at the top of the `z`-tower. This is the engine of `(SURJ-mod-s)`.
