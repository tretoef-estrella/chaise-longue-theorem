# CHAISE LONGUE — THE WALL BAND REDUCTION THEOREM
## The wall `ann_Λ(g^{[q]}) ⊆ c̄` on its first band `q ≤ c < q+k²` is reduced, for every `k`, to two named lemmas: (L1) a box lemma on one sheet, (L2) a gluing lemma on the flat arrangement — and the wall's `q`-dependence is identified: it is exactly the Koszul boundary freedom of degree `c − q`

## §0 · Statements
Setting as in `WALL_VALUE_THEOREM_v2` §0 and `FR_MURO_1` §A. `Λ = R/ℓ^{[q]}R`; `ann = ann_Λ(g^{[q]})`; `c̄` = image of the conductor; band `q ≤ c < q+k²`, `q > k²`.

> **Theorem 1 (sheet-wise solution set, band, ∀k).** On every sheet the witness identity `x|_J·g_i^q|_J = Σ_j ℓ_j^q|_J y_{ij}|_J` is solvable for EVERY `x|_J`, and its solutions are exactly `y_{ij}|_J = Γ^{J,i}_j·x|_J + Σ_{j'} ℓ_{j'}^q|_J·a^{J,i}_{jj'}` with `a^{J,i}` antisymmetric of degree `c−q`. Below the band the boundary term has negative degree and vanishes: Slap 4's theorem is the statement "no room for boundaries". **The wall's `q`-dependence is the boundary freedom.**
> **Theorem 2 (what global witnesses force on a flat, ∀k).** On a flat `F` with syzygy covector `φ_F` (`Σ_j φ_jℓ_j|_F = 0`) and `Γ^{J,i} − Γ^{J',i} = κ_i^Fφ_F`: (i) `x|_F ∈ ℓ^{[q]}K[F]`, hence `x|_F = Σ_j ℓ_j^q|_F ξ_{F,j}` with `ξ_F ∈ K[F]^{k+1}_{c−q}` unique modulo `φ_F`; (ii) `(a^{J',i} − a^{J,i})|_F = κ_i^F(φ_Fξ_F^T − ξ_Fφ_F^T)`.
> **Theorem 3 (reduction, ∀k).** `(I-BAND) ⟸ (L1) ∧ (L2)`:
> **(L1)** in the box `A_J = K[u]/(u^{[q]})`, an element of degree `c` in the band whose restriction to every swap hyperplane `H = {u_{p'} = σu_p}` lies in `(t^q, u_C^{[q]})` belongs to `Δ_J·A_J`;
> **(L2)** flat coefficients `ξ_F` (mod `φ_F`) satisfying (ii) for `i = 1..k` come from ONE global `w ∈ Q^{k+1}_{c−q}` (`Q_{c−q} = R_{c−q}` in the band).
> **Proposition 4 (`c = q`).** `(I)` at `c = q` ⟺ `ω_G = 0` (no `g`-component in the pure-power part of `x`) and `x^{np} ∈ c_`; (ii) becomes a `Λ²K^{k+1}`-valued 1-cocycle condition on the sheet graph, `Σ_{F∈cycle} κ_i^F(φ_F∧ξ_F) = 0`, which by WALL_GLUING_REDUCTION Thm 1 need only be checked on `T`, `Q6`, `Q8`. The first instance to decide: `x = g_{i'}^q`.
> **Proposition 5 (`k = 1`).** (L1) holds (bijectivity `Δ·A_{q−2} → A_q`); (L2) is one scalar condition `Σ_F κ^F X_F = 0` on the triangle, which is `ω_G = 0`; consistent with `ann = c̄` at `k = 1` (FR_CAMBIOS_2).

Grades: Thm 1-3, Prop 4-5 — PROBADO ∀k (pencil). (L1), (L2) — ABIERTO for `k ≥ 2`. No counterexample found; no number, no code.

## §2 · Fable's report, verbatim
## 1 · PRIMER RESULTADO (de una linea, a partir del bancado)
**R1 (from B1 + B4; bookkeeping written out).** For `q ≤ c < q+k²` (and `q > k²`, so that `c < 2q`) the sheet identity `x|_J·g_i^q|_J = Σ_j ℓ_j^q|_J·y_{ij}|_J` has total degree `c+q ∈ [2q, 2q+k²)`, so a monomial carries at most TWO heavy slots (exponent `≥ q`), and a two-heavy monomial `u_m^q u_{m'}^q·(rest)` has `deg(rest) = c−q < k²`. With `g_i^q|_J = Σ_m γ^{J,i}_m u_m^q` (`γ^{J,i} = Γ^{J,i}B_J`), `x|_J = Σ_m u_m^q x_m + x^∅`, `y_{ij}|_J = Σ_m u_m^q y_{ij,m} + y^∅_{ij}` (`x_m, y_{ij,m}` of degree `c−q < q`; `x^∅, y^∅` with all exponents `< q`), the identity splits by heavy-slot pattern into three independent systems and there are NO cross terms (a product `u_{m'}^q·x^∅` has exactly one heavy slot, `m'`; `x^∅` cannot supply a second):
- **(S1) one heavy slot, exponent in `[q,2q)`:** `γ^{J,i}_m x^∅ = Σ_j β^J_{jm} y^∅_{ij}` ∀`i,m` — Slap 4's system on `x^∅`;
- **(S1′) one heavy slot, exponent `≥ 2q`:** `γ^{J,i}_m x_m = Σ_j β^J_{jm} y_{ij,m}` ∀`i,m`;
- **(S2) two heavy slots `m ≠ m'`:** `γ^{J,i}_{m'} x_m + γ^{J,i}_m x_{m'} = Σ_j (β^J_{jm'} y_{ij,m} + β^J_{jm} y_{ij,m'})`.
(S1) with `B_J` invertible forces `y^∅_{ij} = c^J_{ij} x^∅` exactly as in B4. (S1′) does NOT force `y_{ij,m} = c^J_{ij}x_m`: for fixed `i` it is one equation per `m` on a vector that depends on `m`, not `k+1` equations on one vector (witness: `x = ℓ_1^q`, `y_{i1} = g_i^q`, which satisfies (S1′)+(S2) with `y_{ij,m} ≠ c_{ij}x_m`). What (S1′)+(S2) say together is in R2.
## 2 · LO QUE CAE (PROBADO / CANDIDATO / NO LO TENGO)
**R2 (PROBADO ∀k, band) — the exact sheet-wise solution set.** For each `i`, put `v^{(m)} := (y_{ij,m} − c^J_{ij}x_m)_j` and `W^{(i)}_{m m'} := (B_J^T v^{(m)})_{m'}`. Then (S1′) ⟺ `W_{mm} = 0` and (S2) ⟺ `W_{mm'} + W_{m'm} = 0`: **`W^{(i)}` is an antisymmetric `(k+1)×(k+1)` matrix of polynomials of degree `c−q`, otherwise free.** Equivalently (Koszul form): on every sheet, the sheet identity is solvable for ANY `x|_J`, and its solutions are exactly
**`y_{ij}|_J = Γ^{J,i}_j·x|_J + Σ_{j'} ℓ_{j'}^q|_J·a^{J,i}_{jj'}`, `a^{J,i}` antisymmetric of degree `c−q`**
(the tuple `y_{ij} − Γ^{J,i}_j x` is a Koszul cycle of `ℓ^{[q]}|_J`, a regular sequence up to `B_J`, hence a boundary; the boundary's coefficient matrix has degree `c−q ≥ 0` precisely because `c ≥ q` — below the band it is forced to be `0`, which is Slap 4). So the two-heavy system forces nothing on `x` sheet-wise; everything is in the globality of `y` (B1's fenced trap).

**R3 (PROBADO ∀k, band) — what "`y` global" forces on a flat.** On `F = V_J ∩ V_{J'}` (coordinates `(t,u_C)`, `dim F = k`), the `k+1` forms `ℓ_j|_F` are dependent: `Σ_j φ_j ℓ_j|_F = 0` with `φ = φ_F ∈ K^{k+1}` the unique (up to scalar) syzygy — the covector `B_J^{-T}v_F^J` of FR_PUREZA_4; by Frobenius (`φ_j ∈ F_p`) also `Σ_j φ_j ℓ_j^q|_F = 0`. Since `g_i|_F = Σ_j c^J_{ij}ℓ_j|_F = Σ_j c^{J'}_{ij}ℓ_j|_F`: **`Γ^{J,i} − Γ^{J',i} = κ_i^F·φ_F`** with scalars `κ_i^F`, and B4's frame condition ("`x` vanishes on every swap hyperplane") is exactly `κ_i^F ≠ 0` for some `i`, on every flat. Now `y_{ij}|_J|_F = y_{ij}|_{J'}|_F` with R2 gives
**`κ_i^F φ_j · x|_F = Σ_{j'} ℓ_{j'}^q|_F · d^{(i)}_{jj'}`, `d^{(i)} := (a^{J',i} − a^{J,i})|_F` antisymmetric.**
Consequences: (i) the right side lies in `ℓ^{[q]}K[F] = (t^q, u_C^{[q]})` (the `ℓ_j|_F` span `F^*`), so the light part of `x|_F` (all exponents `< q` in `(t,u_C)`) is `0`: **`x|_F ∈ ℓ^{[q]}K[F]` on every flat.** Write `x|_F = Σ_j ℓ_j^q|_F ξ_{F,j}`, `ξ_F ∈ K[F]^{k+1}_{c−q}` unique modulo `φ_F·K[F]`. (ii) Solving the antisymmetric equation (`B_F d = −κ_i X φ^T` with `B_F φ = 0`; the homogeneous antisymmetric solutions `φρ^T = −ρφ^T` are `0` in `char ≠ 2`): **`(a^{J',i} − a^{J,i})|_F = κ_i^F (φ_F ξ_F^T − ξ_F φ_F^T)`** exactly, on every flat and every `i`. Sanity: if `x = Σ_j ℓ_j^q w_j` with `w` global then `ξ_F = w|_F`, and `a^{J,i} := −Γ^{J,i}∧w|_J` satisfies (ii) since `Γ^{J,i} − Γ^{J',i} = κ_i^Fφ_F` — consistent with `y_{ij} = g_i^q w_j`. ✓

**R4 (PROBADO ∀k, band) — what (i) says on a sheet.** The pure-power part `Σ_m x_m u_m^q` of `x|_J` restricts to heavy monomials on every flat, so (i) is a condition on `x^∅` alone: for every pair `(p,p')` and both `σ`, the part of `x^∅` with `deg_{u_p}+deg_{u_{p'}} < q` vanishes on `{u_{p'} = σu_p}`, i.e. **is divisible by `(u_p² − u_{p'}²)`**; the part with `deg_p + deg_{p'} ≥ q` (which becomes `t`-heavy on that flat) is not seen by that flat. Below the band (`c < q`) the second part is empty and one gets `Δ_J | x^∅` directly (B4). In the band the "high" part of each pair is exactly the new phenomenon; note that if `deg_p + deg_{p'} ≥ q` for a monomial, then `deg_m + deg_{m'} ≤ c−q < k²` for every pair disjoint from `{p,p'}`, so such a monomial is "low" (and constrained) on all other disjoint pairs.

**R5 (PROBADO ∀k, band) — reduction of (I-BAND) to two named lemmas.** The goal `x ∈ c_ + ℓ^{[q]}R` is `x̄ ∈ ℓ^{[q]}Q` in the collar (`c_ ⊂ R`, `Q = R/c_`), i.e. a GLOBAL `w ∈ Q^{k+1}_{c−q}` with `x̄ = Σ_j ℓ_j^q w_j`; and since `c_` starts in degree `k(k+1) > k² > c−q`, `Q_{c−q} = R_{c−q}`. R3 gives its local shadow on every flat (`ξ_F`) plus the coboundary condition (ii). So:
**(I-BAND) ⟸ (L1) ∧ (L2)**, where
- **(L1, per sheet, box algebra):** in `A_J = K[u^J]/(u^{[q]})`, an element of degree `c` in the band whose restriction to every swap hyperplane `H` of the sheet lies in `(t^q, u_C^{[q]})` belongs to `Δ_J·A_J` — a statement purely about the box and the `k(k+1)` hyperplanes;
- **(L2, gluing):** the flat coefficients `ξ_F` (mod `φ_F`) of an `x̄ ∈ Q_c` satisfying (ii) for `i = 1..k` come from one global `w ∈ Q^{k+1}_{c−q}`.
Grade: the reduction is PROBADO; (L1), (L2): NO LO TENGO. Everyday version: (L1) is "a sheet that hides on every crease is a multiple of the crease-product"; (L2) is "the shadows of one global thing on all the creases are consistent, and consistency plus the `k` extra equations pin the thing".

**R6 (PROBADO structure, `c = q`) — the lowest degree, where everything is constants.** Write `x = Σ_i ω_i x_i^q + x^{np}` in `S_q` (`ω ∈ K^n`, `x^{np}` with no pure `q`-th power). Since `K^n = K·1 ⊕ L ⊕ G` (`e_1`, the frame, the `g`'s), decompose `ω = ω_1·1 + ω_L + ω_G`; `e_1^q ≡ 0` in `R` and `Σ_{ω_L} ω_ix_i^q ∈ span(ℓ_j^q) = ℓ^{[q]}R_0`. So **(I-BAND) at `c = q` ⟺ `ω_G = 0` and `x^{np} ∈ c_`**: exactly `k` global conditions on the pure part (one per `g_i`, matching the `k` equations of B1) plus the per-sheet conductor condition. In R3 at `c = q` the `ξ_F ∈ K^{k+1}/Kφ_F` and `a^{J,i} ∈ Λ²K^{k+1}` are constants, and (ii) is a genuine cocycle condition on the sheet graph: `Σ_{F ∈ cycle} κ_i^F (φ_F∧ξ_F) = 0` for every cycle (equivalently on T, Q6, Q8 by FR_PUREZA_3 R2) — with `ξ_F` the `ℓ`-coefficients of the pure part of `x|_F`, which is `Σ_i ω_i x_i^q|_F` plus the `t^q`-terms produced by `x^{np}|_F`. For `x = g_{i'}^q` (`ω = ω_G`) this cochain is `κ_i^F φ_F∧Γ^{J,i'}` up to the `φ`-ambiguity, and whether it is a coboundary is exactly whether `g_{i'}^q ∈ ann` — the first case to decide; I did not close it by pencil (it is the `Λ²`-valued cocycle problem on the sheet graph with the frame's `Γ` data).

**R7 (control `k = 1`).** Band = `{c = q}`. Flats are lines, `K[F] = K[t]`, so (i) is vacuous (`x|_F = X_F t^q`); `Λ²K² = K·ω_0`, `a^J = α_J ω_0`, and (ii) reads `α_{J'} − α_J = κ^F det(φ_F, ξ_F) = κ^F X_F` on each of the three edges; around the triangle: **`Σ_F κ^F X_F = 0` (oriented)** — one linear condition on `x`. Independently, `R_q/(c_ + ℓ^{[q]}R)_q` is one-dimensional at `k=1` (`ω ∈ K^4/K·1`, three dimensions, minus `dim L = 2`; and `x^{np}` is automatically in `c_`: `Δ·A_{q−2} → A_q` is bijective for two variables, `dim q−1` both sides, injective by the telescoping identity `Δ·Σ_j u_1^{q−2−2j}u_2^{2j} = u_1^q − u_1u_2^{q−1} ≠ 0`). Since `ann ⊆ {(ii)}` and `ann = c̄` has codimension one (B5), the condition `Σ κ^F X_F = 0` must be exactly `ω_G = 0`; the argument claims nothing beyond B5 and does not contradict it. ✓ (L1) holds at `k=1` by the bijectivity just shown; (L2) at `k=1` is the single condition above.
## 3 · DONDE SE ATASCA
At two named lemmas, both stated exactly (R5). (L1) is local to one sheet and to the box `K[u]/(u^{[q]})`: whether "invisible on every swap hyperplane, in the box sense" forces `Δ_J`-divisibility in the band. Verified at `k=1` (R7). For `k ≥ 2` the high parts of R4 (monomials with `deg_p + deg_{p'} ≥ q`) are what a pencil cannot settle in one turn. (L2) is the global gluing: the cocycle condition (ii) on the sheet graph with values in `Λ²K^{k+1}` (over `K[F]_{c−q}`), for `k` independent `i`'s, must force the local coefficients `ξ_F` to come from one global `w`. At `k=1` it is a single scalar condition and matches B5. What I did NOT do: reconstruct WALL_VALUE / SLAP4's internals, or measure anything. I also did not find a death witness: the natural candidate `x = g_{i'}^q` at `c = q` is a non-member candidate (its cochain `κ_i^F φ_F ∧ Γ^{J,i'}` need not be a coboundary), not a counterexample; deciding it is the first concrete instance of (L2).

## §3 · Auditor's check
R1: heavy-slot split with no cross terms ✓ (`c+q < 2q+k² < 3q` for `q > k²`… the bound needed is `c+q < 3q`, i.e. `c < 2q`, which holds in the band since `k² < q`). (S1) reproduces Slap 4 ✓; `x = ℓ_1^q, y_{i1} = g_i^q` kills the naive extension of Lemma 4.1 ✓. R2: the kernel of `(y_j) ↦ Σℓ_j^q y_j` on a sheet is the Koszul cycles of a regular sequence (the `ℓ_j^q|_J` are a basis change of `u^{[q]}`) = boundaries ✓; degree bookkeeping `c−q` ✓. R3: `φ_F ∈ F_p` so Frobenius preserves the syzygy ✓; `Γ − Γ' ∈ Kφ` ✓; `κ_i ≠ 0` for some `i` is the SEP/transversality condition (discharged by FRAME_DESCENT for the existence of frames; here it is part of "admissible") ✓; (i) and (ii) ✓; the homogeneous antisymmetric solutions vanish in `char ≠ 2` ✓; sanity check with a global `w` ✓. R4-R5: the reduction is a correct chain of implications; the converse is not claimed ✓. R6-R7 ✓. Hypotheses: `char ≠ 2`, frames over `F_p`, `q > k²`.

## §4 · Placement and machinery (auditor)
1. **The wall has a shape now.** `(I)` says: the sheet-wise Koszul boundaries `a^{J,i}` that appear as soon as `c ≥ q` can always be chosen to come from a global `w` (then `y = g^q w` up to global boundaries). Before this turn the corpus said "the proof must be `q`-dependent"; now the `q`-dependence is one object: the degree `c−q` of the boundary term.
2. **(L1) is of the species of (P).** It is a statement in one box `K[u]/(u^{[q]})` about monomial supports and the `k(k+1)` swap hyperplanes; the tools that proved (P) (per-pair restriction, "a monomial that misses a pair", `char ≠ 2`) are the natural tools. Per-pair consequence already on file: the pair-low part of `x^∅` is divisible by `u_p² − u_{p'}²` (R4). What remains is the pair-high part (monomials with `deg_p + deg_{p'} ≥ q`), which is low on every disjoint pair. Mission 8.
3. **(L2) is Čech on the flat arrangement, with the cycle space already generated.** A global `w ∈ K[Y]^{k+1}` is a family of `w|_F` agreeing on the codim-2 intersections `F ∩ F'`; (L2) asks that the cocycle condition (ii) (which lives on the sheet graph) imply that agreement. At `c = q` the data are constants and (ii) is a `Λ²K^{k+1}`-valued 1-cocycle on `Γ_k` — by WALL_GLUING_REDUCTION Thm 1 it suffices to check `T`, `Q6`, `Q8`, and in charts `φ_F`, `κ_i^F` are explicit in `E_J` (WALL_GLUING Thm 2). This is the same machine that closed the wall value. External language: Čech cohomology of subspace arrangements (Goresky–MacPherson; Björner–Ekedahl) — the nerve of the flats `Y`; and `H_1(ℓ^{[q]};Q) = 0` in the band (GLUED_PURITY Thm 2) is exactly the vanishing that lets the witnesses glue once `w` exists (Fable's last abandoned line).
4. **What closes with (L1) ∧ (L2):** `ᾱ_c = (2k+1)!!·C(c−k²,k)` for `c < q+k²` in every dimension (T′ on the annihilator side, ∀k), i.e. the Top zone plus `k²` collar degrees exact ∀k; and the first crack in the wall with `k` inside.
