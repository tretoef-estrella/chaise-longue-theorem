## THEOREM (The Bridge Law, every k — Frobenius' one-paragraph count)
Let Λ_k = S/(E + (ℓ₁^q, …, ℓ_{k+1}^q)) be the ceiling CI (turno 6), with the frame chosen so that (e₁, ℓ₁, …, ℓ_{k+1}) are linearly independent. The surjection Λ_k ↠ S/(E + m^{[q]}) has kernel generated by the classes [s_i^q]. By **Frobenius linearity** ((Σaᵢsᵢ)^q = Σaᵢsᵢ^q for aᵢ ∈ F₃): the frame relations kill k+1 of them ([ℓⱼ^q] = 0) and **e₁ ∈ E kills one more** ([e₁^q] = [Σsᵢ^q] = 0). Hence the span of the [sᵢ^q] has dimension ≤ n − (k+1) − 1 = **k**, and completing (e₁, ℓ's) to a basis by g₁, …, g_k:
> **ker(Λ_k ↠ A-ring) = (g₁^q, …, g_k^q)·Λ_k — exactly k generators, for every k and every q.** ∎
**Verified byte-exact at THREE dimensions:** span of the powers in Λ = 2, 3, 4 at k = 2, 3, 4 exactly ✓✓✓ (and k=2 is the Sofa's sealed law).

## COROLLARY (The Two-Column Law ∀k∀q — pillar iii, parametrized)
> **A_k(q) = (2k+1)!!·q^{k+1} − length_Λ(g₁^q, …, g_k^q) = dim ann_Λ(g₁^q, …, g_k^q).**
The entire object of the conjecture is the annihilator of k Frobenius powers in one explicit complete intersection.

## THE ENDGAME IDENTITY (the single remaining statement of the whole Chaise Longue)
With the sandwich (turnos 5-6) and the bridge (today):
> **A_k(q) = P_k(q) ∀k∀q ⟺ length_Λ(g₁^q, …, g_k^q) = D_k(q) := (2k+1)!!·q^{k+1} − P_k(q)** — an explicit polynomial by the Floor Theorem.
Anchor: k=2, q=3: D₂(3) = 405 − 141 = **264 = the Sofa's SEALED bridge length** ✓✓ (Ley 39). Target k=3, q=3: D₃(3) = 8505 − 1107 = 7398. Since A ≥ P is a theorem (Floor Bound), the identity reduces to ONE inequality: **length ≥ D_k(q)** (the bridge ideal is at least as long as the gap).
