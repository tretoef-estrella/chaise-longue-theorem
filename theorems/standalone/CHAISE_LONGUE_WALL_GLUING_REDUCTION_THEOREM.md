# CHAISE LONGUE — THE WALL GLUING REDUCTION THEOREM
## `H_1(ℓ^{[q]};Q)_{q+k²} ≠ 0` for every `k` ⟺ two local holonomy identities on six and eight points — with the cycle space of the sheet graph generated by triangles, 6-point quadrangles and 8-point squares, and the frame scalar `λ_F` written explicitly

## §0 · Statements
**Setting** as in `GLUED_PURITY_THEOREM_v1` §0. Sheet graph `Γ_k`: vertices the `(2k+1)!!` matchings, edges the swaps (each vertex has `k(k+1)` neighbours; each edge = one codim-1 flat `F`). For the wall family `z^J_p = c_J·∏_{m≠p}u_m` the gluing condition `(E2')` is the rank-one connection `c_{J'} = μ_F c_J`, `μ_F = λ_F σ_J/σ_{J'}`.

> **Theorem 1 (generators of the cycle space, ∀k).** `H_1(Γ_k; Z)` is generated by three local types of cycles: **T** (the three matchings of four points, same `C`), **Q6** (`(ab)(cd)(ef)C → (cb)(ad)(ef)C → (eb)(ad)(cf)C → (eb)(cd)(af)C → back`), **Q8** (two commuting swaps on disjoint 4-point sets). Proof: `Cay(S_{2k+2}, transpositions) → Γ_k` is a covering with deck group `Stab(J) = B_{k+1}`; lift the three relators of `S_n = ⟨t_{xy} | t², [t_{xy},t_{zw}], t_{xy}t_{yz}t_{xy}t_{xz}⟩` and do the casework on how `J` meets the 3–4 points.
> **Theorem 2 (reduction, ∀k).** `μ`-holonomy is a homomorphism `H_1(Γ_k;Z) → K^*`, invariant under sign changes of sheet coordinates. Hence **A′-EXISTENCE ⟺ Hol(T) = 1 ∧ Hol(Q6) = 1 ∧ Hol(Q8) = 1.** `Hol(T) = 1` is PROBADO (GLUED_PURITY Thm 3 + hinge signs). The two remaining identities `(I-Q6)`, `(I-Q8)` are written on the object in §2 R3. `N = 2`, independent of `k`.
> **Proposition 3 (the `λ_F` formula; definition unpacked).** With `B_J = (β^J_{jm})` the sheet matrix (`ℓ_j|_{V_J} = Σ_m β^J_{jm}u_m`) and `M_{J→J'} := B_{J'}^T B_J^{-T}`: for the flat `F = H_{pp'σ}` of `J`, merging slots `r,r'` of `J'`,
> **`M_{J→J'}(e_p − σe_{p'}) = λ_F·(e_r − σ'e_{r'})`** — the vector on the right is forced (the form vanishes on `F`); `λ_F` is its coefficient. Moreover `M_{J'→J''}M_{J→J'} = M_{J→J''}`, so the transition connection `M` itself has trivial holonomy on every cycle; the holonomy of `μ` is the product of the scalars by which `M` carries the FLAT VECTORS `v_F^J := e_p − σe_{p'}` of successive sheets.
> **Proposition 4 (`k = 2`).** `Γ_2` is the line graph of the generalized quadrangle `W(2) = GQ(2,2)` (points = 15 pairs of a 6-set, lines = 15 matchings), which by self-duality is its collinearity graph (Kneser `K(6,2)`, spectrum `6, 1^9, (−3)^5`, `Aut = S_6`). Triangles = lines of the GQ (15); the quadrangles `Q6` are NOT in the span of the triangles (`b_1` of the triangle-filled complex `≥ 16`). So `Hol(Q6) = 1` is new information about the frame, not a consequence of `Hol(T) = 1`.

Grades: Thm 1, Thm 2, Prop 3, Prop 4 — PROBADO with `k` as a letter (Prop 4 at `k=2` by construction). `Hol(Q6) = 1`, `Hol(Q8) = 1`: ABIERTO (mission 4). `Lemma L` (locality of `λ_F` in `C`): NAMED, not proved, and NOT expected to hold as stated (see §5).

## §2 · Fable's report, verbatim
## 1 · PRIMER RESULTADO (de una linea, a partir del bancado)
**R1 (PROBADO ∀k, from B1 + B3 with G ≡ 0).** A family with `G ≡ 0` on every leaf, `z^J_p = c_J ∏_{m≠p} u_m`, is a glued cycle at `d = k` if and only if `c_{J'} = μ_F c_J` on every flat `F`, `μ_F = λ_F σ_J/σ_{J'}` — a rank-one connection on the sheet graph; it has a nonzero solution iff the holonomy of `μ` is `+1` around every cycle of the sheet graph. (Note: (Z) holds automatically for this family, since `∏_{m≠p}u_m|_H = σ·∏_{m≠p'}u_m|_H` on `H = {u_{p'} = σ u_p}`.)
## 2 · LO QUE CAE (PROBADO / CANDIDATO / NO LO TENGO)
**R2 (PROBADO ∀k) — the cycle space of the sheet graph is generated by three local types.**
Setup: the sheet graph `Γ_k` is the Schreier graph of `S_{2k+2}` acting on perfect matchings, generators = all transpositions `t_{xy}` (`t_{xy}` fixes `J` iff `{x,y} ∈ J` → loop, dropped; `t_{ac}` and `t_{bd}` give the same edge `(ab)(cd) — (ad)(bc)` → parallel edges identified; the simple graph has `2·C(k+1,2) = k(k+1)` neighbours per vertex, matching B0).
Fact used (standard, proof in two lines): `S_n = ⟨ t_{xy} | t_{xy}² , [t_{xy},t_{zw}] (disjoint) , t_{xy}t_{yz}t_{xy} = t_{xz} ⟩`. (The group so presented surjects onto `S_n`; the third relation writes every `t_{xy}` as a word in adjacent transpositions and yields the braid relations, so it is also a quotient of the Coxeter group `S_n`.)
Covering argument: `Cay(S_{2k+2}, T) → Γ_k` is a covering with deck group `Stab(J) = B_{k+1}`; hence `H_1(Γ_k;Z)` is generated by (a) the lifts of the three relators at every vertex, and (b) closed walks at one vertex spelling generators of `B_{k+1}` (pair transpositions = loops; pair swaps `t_{ac}t_{bd}` = the walk `J→J'→J` on one edge = backtrack). So only (a) contributes. Casework on how `J` meets the 3 or 4 points of a relator:
- `t_{xy}t_{yz}t_{xy}t_{xz}`: `{x,y}∈J` or `{y,z}∈J` → **triangle T** (the three matchings of 4 points, same `C`); `{x,z}∈J` → backtrack; `x,y,z` in three pairs of `J` → **quadrangle Q6**.
- `[t_{xy},t_{zw}]`: two pairs among the 4 points → loops or backtrack; one pair of type `{x,z}` etc. → **Q6**; no pair → **square Q8**.
Explicit models (`C` = the untouched pairs, common to all vertices of the cycle):
- **T**: `(ab)(cd)C — (ad)(bc)C — (ac)(bd)C — (ab)(cd)C`.
- **Q6**: `J=(ab)(cd)(ef)C → (cb)(ad)(ef)C → (eb)(ad)(cf)C → (eb)(cd)(af)C → J`. Opposite vertices share no pair on the six points; four distinct sheets; genuine 4-cycle.
- **Q8**: `J=A·B·D·E·C`, `J^{AB}` (swap of pairs A,B, sign σ), `J^{AB,DE}`, `J^{DE}`, back to `J`: two commuting swaps on disjoint 4-point sets.
Consequence: `μ`-holonomy is a homomorphism `H_1(Γ_k;Z) → K^*`, gauge-invariant under sign changes of the sheet coordinates (a sign change of `u^J_m` rescales `c_J` by `±1` and conjugates `μ`; holonomy unchanged), so A′-EXISTENCE is **equivalent** to: Hol(T)=1 [B4, PROBADO], Hol(Q6)=1, Hol(Q8)=1. Filter: `k` is a letter; N = 2 remaining identities, N independent of `k`. Control `k=1`: `Γ_1` = one triangle, only T exists — nothing here touches it. `Q6` first appears at `k=2`, `Q8` at `k=3`.
Sharpening (CANDIDATO): at `k=2` (`Γ_2` = collinearity graph of GQ(2,2): 15 vertices, 45 edges, cycle rank 31, only 15 triangles) the quadrangles are NOT in the span of the triangles (Euler count of the triangle-filled complex: `15−45+15 = −15` ⇒ `b_1 ≥ 16 > 0`). So Hol(Q6)=1 cannot follow from B4 alone by any cycle-space argument; it is new information about `λ`. (CANDIDATO only because I have not checked that every single Q6 is non-trivial mod triangles; the count shows most are.)
**R3 (PROBADO ∀k, bookkeeping) — the two identities, written on the object.** With `μ_F c_J = c_{J'}` on every flat and the models of R2:
- **(I-Q8)** `μ(J→J^{AB}) · μ(J^{AB}→J^{AB,DE}) = μ(J→J^{DE}) · μ(J^{DE}→J^{AB,DE})`.
- **(I-Q6)** `μ(J→J_1) · μ(J_1→J_2) · μ(J_2→J_3) · μ(J_3→J) = 1` with `J=(ab)(cd)(ef)C, J_1=(cb)(ad)(ef)C, J_2=(eb)(ad)(cf)C, J_3=(eb)(cd)(af)C`.
Both are statements about `λ` (the `σ`-factors are ±1 and computable per flat once the slot convention of (E2′) is fixed; `μ` itself is convention-free, see R2).
**R4 (NO LO TENGO) — the value of `λ_F`.** The DIANA banks `λ_F` only as "a nonzero frame scalar" with the triangle relation B4. Neither identity can be evaluated from that. This is the single missing input; nothing else is missing.
**R5 (PROBADO under Lemma L) — Q8 closes if `λ` is local.** *Lemma L (named, not proved):* `λ_F` depends only on the four points of the swap, the sign `σ`, and the frame — not on the pairs `C` that the two sheets share. Under L, in Q8 the flats `J—J^{AB}` and `J^{DE}—J^{AB,DE}` are the same 4-point swap with the same `σ` (they differ only in `C`), so `μ(J→J^{AB}) = μ(J^{DE}→J^{AB,DE})`, likewise for the `DE` swap, and (I-Q8) holds termwise. Under L, (I-Q6) is a single identity on six points with `C` as inert parameter — i.e. a `k=2` statement that is then automatically ∀k.
**R6 (PROBADO ∀k, reformulation).** Under L: A′-EXISTENCE ⇔ (I-Q6) at `k=2` for every admissible frame on 6 points. Without L: A′-EXISTENCE ⇔ (I-Q6) ∧ (I-Q8) with `C` live in both.

## 3 · DONDE SE ATASCA
Exactly at `λ_F`. Every step from B1–B4 to "holonomy on a generating set" is done (R2, R3); the generating set is three local types and one of them is already banked (B4). What stops the closing is that the two remaining identities are identities in `λ`, and `λ` is banked only by name. Without its expression in the frame (or at least its dependence on `C` — Lemma L) I cannot prove, refute, or even reduce (I-Q6). I did not attempt to reconstruct `λ` (prohibited, and not in the DIANA).
What would close it: (1) prove Lemma L from the definition of `λ_F` → (I-Q8) PROBADO; (2) evaluate (I-Q6) once, on `J=(ab)(cd)(ef)`, with `λ` in hand → A′-EXISTENCE PROBADO ∀k, or a counter-cycle (the deliverable of the death criterion). Both are one-line computations once `λ` is a formula; neither is available to me here.

## §3 · The `λ_F` formula (auditor; definition unpacking)
`L_F^{(J)} := Σ_j a_j ℓ_j` is the frame element restricting to `u_p − σu_{p'}` on `V_J`. Since `Σ_j a_jℓ_j|_{V_J} = Σ_m (B_J^T a)_m u_m`, this is `B_J^T a = e_p − σe_{p'}`, i.e. `a = B_J^{-T}(e_p − σe_{p'})`. Its restriction to `V_{J'}` is `Σ_m (B_{J'}^T a)_m u'_m`. That form vanishes on `F = V_J ∩ V_{J'}`, so on `V_{J'}` it is a multiple of `u'_r − σ'u'_{r'}`: `B_{J'}^T B_J^{-T}(e_p − σe_{p'}) = λ_F (e_r − σ'e_{r'})`. This is exactly the `λ_F` of `FR_PUREZA_2` R3 (`L^{(J')}_F = λ_F L^{(J)}_F`, both normalised on their own sheet). Composition: `M_{J'→J''}M_{J→J'} = B_{J''}^T B_{J'}^{-T} B_{J'}^T B_J^{-T} = M_{J→J''}` — trivial holonomy of `M`; the non-trivial content of `Hol(μ)` is entirely in how the flat vectors `v_F` of consecutive sheets relate under `M`, times the hinge signs `σ_J/σ_{J'}` (from `∏_{m≠p}u_m|_F = σ_J t ∏_C u`).
Consequence for Lemma L: `λ_F` depends on `M_{J→J'}`, i.e. on both full sheet matrices — the shared pairs `C` enter through `B_J`, `B_{J'}`. Locality in `C` is therefore NOT automatic; it is a genuine claim about the frame, to be proved or refuted with this formula (mission 4).

## §4 · Auditor's check
Theorem 1: the presentation of `S_n` by transpositions with the three relators is standard (Coxeter presentation follows by writing `t_{xy}` as conjugates of adjacent transpositions); the covering `Cay → Γ_k` with deck group `Stab(J)` and the generation of `H_1` of the quotient by lifted relators plus stabilizer words is the Reidemeister–Schreier argument ✓; the stabilizer words are loops or backtracks ✓; casework re-walked: the relator `t_{xy}t_{yz}t_{xy}t_{xz}` with `x,y,z` in three distinct pairs of `J` gives four distinct matchings on six points — the `Q6` model ✓; `[t_{xy},t_{zw}]` with no pair among the four points gives the commuting square on eight points ✓. Theorem 2: holonomy of a rank-one connection is a homomorphism on `H_1` ✓; gauge invariance under `u^J_m ↦ −u^J_m` ✓. Prop 4: `15 − 45 + 15 = −15 ⇒ b_1 ≥ 16` ✓ (`b_2` of the clique complex could only raise it). Hypotheses: all `B_J` invertible; `char ≠ 2`.

## §5 · Placement
1. The upper half of the `k²` law (`W` glues ∀k) is now `N = 2` FIXED identities in the frame data, with `λ_F` explicit. This replaces "holonomy on every cycle" (a `k`-growing list of cycles) by two local checks: Ley 0 satisfied by Theorem 1.
2. `Q6` lives on six points: it is a `k = 2` object, and `Γ_2 = GQ(2,2)` is a classical geometry with `S_6` symmetry and an outer automorphism. Machinery (external): the doily / Tutte 8-cage; the Kneser graph `K(6,2)`; `Sp(4,2) ≅ S_6`. If `Hol(Q6) = 1` for every admissible frame on six points, and `λ_F` were local in `C`, A′-EXISTENCE would be ∀k from the `k=2` case alone (Fable's R5-R6). §3 shows locality is not free.
3. The control cell `k = 1` is untouched (only `T` exists) ✓.
4. What a proof of `Hol(Q6) = 1` needs: the four `M`-transitions around the `Q6` model applied to the four flat vectors, times four hinge signs — one explicit product in the entries of four `3×3` blocks (six points; the `C`-block factors out or does not — that is Lemma L).
