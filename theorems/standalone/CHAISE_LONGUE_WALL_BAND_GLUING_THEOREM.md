# CHAISE LONGUE — THE WALL BAND GLUING THEOREM
## On the sub-band `q ≤ c < q+(k−1)(k−2)` (indeed for `d = c−q ≤ k(k−1)`), every twist of the witness data vanishes on every joint of the sheet arrangement — no degeneracy condition — so the per-sheet data of an annihilating element form ONE flat-compatible family; and (I) there is EQUIVALENT to a frame-free statement: that flat-compatible families of low degree come from `R` (equivalently, a Hilbert-function identity of the A-ring)

## §0 · Statements
Setting as in `FR_MURO_7` §A: sheets `V_J`, flats `F` (each in exactly two sheets), cores (triangle cores `{u_p = u_{p'} = 0}`, 4-cycle cores `W`), `φ_F`, `κ_i^F`, witnesses `a^{J,i}`; `d = c − q`; `R_d = K[Y]_d` for `d < k(k+1)`.

> **Theorem 1 (correction; the flat condition alone is not enough).** The family `ξ^J = Γ^{J,i}·h` (`h ∈ S_d`) satisfies the flat condition `(ξ^J − ξ^{J'})|_F ∈ φ_F K[F]` on every flat and is NOT global. The correct reduction of (I) is: every per-sheet family satisfying the flat condition AND the witness cocycle `a^{J',i}|_F − a^{J,i}|_F = κ_i^F φ_F ∧ ξ^J|_F` admits a global `w`.
> **Theorem 2 (twists vanish on every core, ∀k ≥ 3, every frame, every `d < q`).** Writing `ξ^{J'}|_F − ξ^J|_F = ρ_F φ_F`: (i) on a triangle core the cocycle summed around the triangle gives, coefficient-wise over `K[u_C]`, a unique `w_T` with `ξ^{J_l}|_L = w_T` on all three sheets, so `ρ_{F_l}|_L = 0`; (ii) on a `Q6`/`Q8` core, with per-sheet data the cocycle for all `κ` in the 2-dimensional relation space yields `a·κ²·r_1 + κ³(c·r_1 + (cb−ad)r_2) = 0`, hence `r_0 = r_1 = r_2 = r_3 = 0` — **with no degeneracy condition**: the (ND) residue of WALL_FIRST_DEGREE is void.
> **Theorem 3 (twists vanish on every flat for `d ≤ k(k−1)`, ∀k ≥ 3).** Inside a flat `F` (dim `k`) lie `1 + 2(k−1) + 2·C(k−1,2) = 1 + k(k−1)` pairwise non-associated lines-of-cores: the corner `{t = 0}`, the `Q6` cores `{u_{p''} = ±t}`, and the `Q8` cores `{u_{q'} = ±u_q}` (crossings of disjoint creases — the Architect's second family of weathervanes). `ρ_F` vanishes on all of them; `deg ρ_F = d ≤ k(k−1)` ⟹ `ρ_F = 0`: **`ξ^J|_F = ξ^{J'}|_F` on every flat.**
> **Theorem 4 (the reduction, sub-band).** With `x' := x − (Δ_J h_J)_J ∈ R_{q+d}`, `x'|_J = Σ_j ℓ_j^q|_J ξ^J_j` on every sheet, and `x ∈ c̄` ⟺ `(ξ^J)_J` is the restriction of a global `w ∈ R_d^{k+1}` ⟺ `[ξ] = 0 ∈ H_1(ℓ^{[q]}; R̃/R)_{q+d}`. Hence **(I) on `q ≤ c < q+(k−1)(k−2)` ⟺ B6′: every flat-compatible family of degree `d` arising from an annihilating `x` lies in `R_d`.** PROBADO modulo B6′.
> **Theorem 5 (frame-free form of (I), every `c`).** `Λ/g^{[q]}Λ = S/(e_1,e_3,…,e_{2k+1}, x^{[q]})` = **the A-ring `A_k(q)` itself** (Frobenius: `(e_1)+ℓ^{[q]}+g^{[q]} = x^{[q]}`), so by Gorenstein duality of `Λ`: `dim ann_c = A_{σ_Λ − c}` in every degree, frame-independent. On the band (`H_1(ℓ^{[q]};Q) = 0`): **(I)_c ⟺ `A_{σ_Λ − c} = (2k+1)!!·hf_{box}(c − k(k+1))`** — the ceiling law exact at the collar degree `σ_Λ − c`.

Grades: Thms 1-3, 5 PROBADO ∀k as stated; Thm 4 PROBADO as a reduction; B6′: ABIERTO.

## §2 · Fable's report, verbatim
## 1 · PRIMER RESULTADO (de una linea, a partir del bancado)
**R1 (from B1 + B2 + B3).** Under (L1) on the sub-band, (I)_c ⟺ (L2′)_d: for every family of per-sheet vectors `ξ'^J ∈ K[u^J]^{k+1}_d` with `(ξ'^J − ξ'^{J'})|_F ∈ φ_F K[F]_d` on every flat, there is a global `w ∈ K[Y]^{k+1}_d` with `(ξ'^J − w)|_F ∈ φ_F K[F]_d` on every flat. The data live on sheets, the conditions on flats; a crossing `F ∩ F'` inside a sheet carries both conditions at once (B5). Two remarks written before thinking further: (a) B3's `w` is a polynomial vector on the union of sheets (`K[Y]_d = R_d`, `d < k(k+1)`), and the condition "`(ξ'^J − w)|_F ∈ φ_F K[F]`" for all flats of `J` says `ξ'^J − w|_{V_J}` lies in the ideal `∩_F (φ_F) `-type module of `V_J`; (b) the family `ξ'^J` is only determined modulo the freedom of B1 (`x'^J` is defined up to what `Δ_J h_J` absorbs), so (L2′) is a statement about classes, not vectors.
## 2 · LO QUE CAE (PROBADO / CANDIDATO / NO LO TENGO)
**R2 (PROBADO, a correction to B4/B7 and to R1) — (L2′) as printed is FALSE at every `d`, and B4's proof did not use B2 alone.** The family `ξ^J := Γ^{J,i}·h` (`h ∈ S_d` any global polynomial, `i` fixed) satisfies B2 on every flat: `(ξ^J − ξ^{J'})|_F = κ_i^F φ_F h|_F ∈ φ_F K[F]_d`. It is the per-sheet family of `x = g_i^q h` (`h_J = 0` in B1). At `d = 0` it is not global: a global `w` would need `w = Γ^{J,i}` on every sheet (`Z_J := {ζ : ζ|_F ∈ φ_FK[F] ∀F ⊂ V_J}` is `∩_F Kφ_F = 0` for constants), and `Γ^{·,i}` is not constant since `g_i ∉ L ⊕ Ke_1`. So the flat condition B2 by itself admits a twist that passes every joint; what excluded it at `c = q` (FR_MURO_3 R2–R6, i.e. WALL_FIRST_DEGREE) was the **witness cocycle**: `a^{J',i}|_F − a^{J,i}|_F = κ_i^F φ_F ∧ ξ^J|_F` with `a^{J,i}` antisymmetric of degree `d` on each sheet (FR_MURO_1 R2–R3), whose triangle version kills `g_i^q`. The correct reduction is therefore: **(I)_c ⟺ every per-sheet family satisfying B2 AND the witness cocycle admits a global `w`.** Everything below uses the cocycle. (For the record, `x = g_i^q h ∉ ann` for `d < k`: the triangle cocycle restricted to the core forces `h|_{L_C} = 0` on every codim-2 coordinate locus, hence `h = 0`.)

**R3 (PROBADO ∀k ≥ 3, every admissible frame, every `d < q`) — all twists vanish on every core.** For a flat `F ⊂ V_J ∩ V_{J'}` write `ξ^{J'}|_F − ξ^J|_F = ρ_F·φ_F`, `ρ_F ∈ K[F]_d` (the twist of the joint). (i) *Triangle cores.* On the triangle of a pair `(p,p')` at `J` the three flats contain the core `L = {u_p = u_{p'} = 0}` (coordinates `u_C`, common to the three sheets). Restrict the cocycle of each flat to `L` and add around the triangle: the `a`'s telescope and `Σ_l κ_i^{F_l} φ_{F_l} ∧ ξ^{J_l}|_L = 0` over `K[u_C]`. The `φ`'s and `κ`'s are constants, so the linear algebra of FR_MURO_3 R3 applies coefficient-wise (base change of a constant map to `K[u_C]`, a free `K`-module): there is a unique `w_T ∈ K[u_C]^{k+1}_d` with `ξ^{J_l}|_L ≡ w_T` mod `φ_{F_l}·K[u_C]` for the three flats; each sheet of the triangle has two of these flats with independent `φ`'s, hence **`ξ^{J_l}|_L = w_T` on all three sheets**, and `ρ_{F_l}|_L = 0`. (ii) *4-cycle cores.* Let `Q` be a `Q6` or `Q8` through `J` with sheets `J_0 = J, J_1, J_2, J_3`, flats `F_l` (from `J_l` to `J_{l+1}`) and common core `W` (codim 2 in each sheet; FR_MURO_3 R4). Put `r_l := ρ_{F_l}|_W ∈ K[W]_d`, so `ξ^{J_l}|_W = ξ^J|_W + Σ_{m<l} r_mφ_m` and `Σ_l r_lφ_l = 0`. With `φ_2 = aφ_0 + bφ_1`, `φ_3 = cφ_0 + dφ_1` (`a ≠ 0`, `d ≠ 0`, `ad − bc ≠ 0`: consecutive flats of a sheet have independent `φ`), the cocycle restricted to `W`, for every `κ` in the 2-dimensional relation space (spanned by the `κ_i`, FR_MURO_3 R4 ii), reads after cancelling the `ξ^J|_W`-terms and dividing by `ω = φ_0∧φ_1`:
**`a·κ^2·r_1 + κ^3·(c·r_1 + (cb − ad)·r_2) = 0` for all `(κ^2, κ^3)`,** hence `r_1 = 0`, then `r_2 = 0`, then `r_0φ_0 + r_3φ_3 = 0` gives `r_0 = r_3 = 0`. No degeneracy condition is needed: with per-sheet data the doubly-degenerate case of FR_MURO_3 (ND) disappears — the unknowns are the twists themselves, not classes mod `φ`. So **`ρ_F` vanishes on every `Q6`/`Q8` core contained in `F`.** (This retroactively removes the (ND) residue of FR_MURO_3 for `c = q`.)

**R4 (PROBADO ∀k ≥ 3, every admissible frame) — the twist of every joint vanishes for `d ≤ k(k−1)`.** Fix `F = H_{pp'σ} ⊂ V_J` (coordinates `t, u_C`). Inside `F` lie: the triangle core `{t = 0}`; the `Q6` cores `{u_{p''} = σ''t}` for every `p'' ∈ C` and both signs (a `Q6` through `J` using `F` and `H_{pp''σ''}` exists in a suitable chart, FR_MURO_3 R5); the `Q8` cores `{u_{q'} = σ'u_q}` for every pair `q < q'` in `C` and both signs (the crossings of B5; `Q8` exists for `k ≥ 3`). By R3, `ρ_F` vanishes on all of them; these are `1 + 2(k−1) + 2·C(k−1,2) = 1 + k(k−1)` pairwise non-associated linear forms of `K[F]`, so their product divides `ρ_F`, and `deg ρ_F = d ≤ k(k−1)` forces **`ρ_F = 0`: `ξ^J|_F = ξ^{J'}|_F` on every flat.** ∎ This covers the whole sub-band `d < (k−1)(k−2)` and more (up to `d = k(k−1)`; the top `k−1` degrees of the band, `d ≥ k² − k + 1`, are not covered). Everyday version: the twist is a polynomial on the crease that must vanish on `1 + k(k−1)` different lines drawn on the crease — the corner line, the lines where the neighbouring creases cut it, and the lines where the creases that never touch it cross it; a polynomial of smaller degree cannot.

**R5 (PROBADO modulo B6, sub-band) — the per-sheet vectors form ONE element of the seminormalisation; (I) reduces to B6's converse.** With `ρ ≡ 0`, the family `(ξ^J)_J ∈ ∏_J K[u^J]^{k+1}_d` agrees on all flats, hence on all pairwise intersections of sheets (any point of `V_J ∩ V_{J''}` lies on a chain of flats between `J` and `J''`). Put `x' := x − (Δ_Jh_J)_J`: since `(Δ_Jh_J)_J ∈ c_ ⊂ R`, **`x' ∈ R_{q+d}` with `x'|_J = Σ_j ℓ_j^q|_J ξ^J_j` on every sheet.** Now `x ∈ c̄_c ⟺ x' ∈ ℓ^{[q]}R ⟺ (ξ^J)_J = w|_{sheets}` for some `w ∈ R_d^{k+1}` (the coefficients are unique: `Σ_jℓ_j^q|_Jζ_j = 0` with `deg ζ < q` forces `ζ = 0`). So **(I)_c ⟺ the flat-compatible family `(ξ^J)_J` of degree `d` lies in `R_d = K[Y]_d`** — exactly B6's converse for THIS family; equivalently, via the connecting map of `0 → R → R̃ → R̃/R → 0`, `x̄' ∈ Λ` is the image of the class `[ξ] ∈ H_1(ℓ^{[q]}; R̃/R)_{q+d}` (`ℓ^{[q]}` is a regular sequence on `R` — `R = S/E` is a complete intersection and the frame is a system of parameters — and on each `K[u^J]`, so `H_1(ℓ^{[q]};R) = H_1(ℓ^{[q]};R̃) = 0`). Graded: **(I) on the sub-band is PROBADO modulo B6** (the converse is needed only for flat-compatible families that come from an `x ∈ ann`; I could not remove it, and I do not assume it).

**R6 (PROBADO ∀k, every `c`) — a frame-free reformulation of (I), new.** `Λ = R/ℓ^{[q]}R` is an Artinian complete intersection (`R` a CI, `ℓ^{[q]}` regular), hence Gorenstein with socle degree `σ = (k+1)(q+k−1)`; for any ideal `I ⊂ Λ`, `dim (0:I)_c = dim (Λ/I)_{σ−c}`. With `I = g^{[q]}Λ`: `Λ/g^{[q]}Λ = S/(E + ℓ^{[q]} + g^{[q]})`, and since `(e_1, ℓ, g)` is a basis of `S_1` and `K = F_p`, Frobenius gives `(e_1) + ℓ^{[q]} + g^{[q]} = (x_1^q, …, x_n^q)`; so **`Λ/g^{[q]}Λ = S/(e_1, e_3, …, e_{2k+1}, x_1^q, …, x_n^q) =: B`, a ring that does not see the frame.** Therefore `dim ann_c = HF_B(σ − c)` is frame-independent in every degree, and with FR_MURO_1 B3 (`dim c̄_c = (2k+1)!!·hf_{box}(c − k(k+1))` on the band):
**(I)_c ⟺ `HF_B((k+1)(q+k−1) − c) = (2k+1)!!·hf_{K[u_1..u_{k+1}]/(u^{[q]})}(c − k(k+1))`** — a purely numerical identity about the odd elementary symmetric functions in the `n`-variable box (the inequality `≥` holds always, since `c̄ ⊆ ann`). Equivalently, by duality, `ann_Λ(c̄) = g^{[q]}Λ` in degree `σ − c`. Grade: PROBADO as an equivalence; the identity itself: NO LO TENGO (no number written). This is the first statement of the wall with `k` a letter and NO frame, NO flats, NO sheets; it is also the natural object to compute rather than to prove.

**R7 (controls).** `d = 0`: R3(i) is FR_MURO_3 R3 verbatim (kernel `3k − (2k−1) = k+1` over `K`), R4 gives `ρ_F = 0` (constants vanishing on a line), R5's family is a constant on every sheet, hence global — B6 vacuous, B4 recovered. ✓ A family that is the restriction of a global `w`: all `ρ_F = 0` identically, R5's element `x'` is `Σ ℓ_j^q w_j`, passes. ✓ The counterexample family `Γ^{J,i}h` of R2 fails the cocycle (R2), as it must.
## 3 · DONDE SE ATASCA
At B6's converse, and nowhere else on the sub-band: a family of degree-`d` polynomial vectors on the sheets, agreeing on every flat (proved, R4), must be the restriction of one element of `R_d = K[Y]_d`. For `d = 0` it is trivial; for `d ≥ 1` it is the seminormality-type question of the arrangement in low degree, which I could not settle by pencil (subspace arrangements can fail it precisely in low degrees: the planar triple point fails already in degree 1). I did not assume it. Two ways out are on the table: (a) prove B6's converse for this arrangement in degrees `d < (k−1)(k−2)`; (b) bypass sheets altogether through R6 — the frame-free Hilbert-function identity, which needs neither B6 nor the cocycle and is a computation on one explicit ring. Not covered by R4: the top `k−1` degrees of the band (`d ≥ k²−k+1`); and `k = 2` (no `Q8`).

## §4 · Placement and machinery
1. **The witness data of an annihilating element are now ONE flat-compatible object on the sheet arrangement** (Thm 3), for `d ≤ k(k−1)` — covering the sub-band and `k−1` more degrees. The remaining question is purely about the arrangement `X = ∪V_J` (frame-free after Thm 5): does a degree-`d` tuple of polynomials on the sheets, agreeing on the flats, come from `R = K[X]`? This is the low-degree seminormality of `X`.
2. **Structure of the residual:** `M := R^+/R`, where `R^+` = flat-compatible tuples, is a graded module killed by the conductor `c_` (since `c_R̃ ⊂ R`), hence a `K[Y]`-module supported on the singular locus; `M_d` for `d < k(k−1)` is what must vanish (on the classes coming from `ann`). Local model: a flat lies in exactly two sheets (normal-crossing-like), a triangle core in exactly three (the "triple point" type where seminormality can fail in degree 1).
3. **Two exits, both frame-free:** (a) prove `M_d = 0` for `d < (k−1)(k−2)` (or that `ann`-classes avoid it) — mission 14; (b) the numerical identity of Thm 5 — a Hilbert function of `A` at the top collar degrees, which the corpus can measure but not prove without (a) or an equivalent.
4. **The weathervane worked:** the `Q8` cores are the crossings; without them the twist has only `1 + 2(k−1)` lines to vanish on and Thm 3 reaches only `d ≤ 2k−2`; with them, `d ≤ k(k−1)`. The second family of vanes bought the quadratic range.
