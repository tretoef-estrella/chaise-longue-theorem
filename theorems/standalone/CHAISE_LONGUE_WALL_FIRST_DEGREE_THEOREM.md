## §0 · Statements
Setting as in `WALL_BAND_REDUCTION_THEOREM_v1` and `FR_MURO_3` §A (`Γ^{J,i}`, `φ_F`, `κ_i^F`, the cocycle condition of a global witness at `c = q`).

> **Theorem 1 (the triangle cocycle collapses, ∀k).** On a four-point triangle with flats `F_0, F_1, F_2`, the cocycle condition for `ξ_F = Γ^{J(F),i'}` reduces to the single term `κ_i^{F_0}κ_{i'}^{F_1}·(φ_{F_0} ∧ φ_{F_1}) = 0`; since consecutive `φ`'s are independent, `κ_i^{F_0}κ_{i'}^{F_1} = 0`, and cyclically.
> **Theorem 2 (`g_{i'}^q ∉ ann`, ∀k ≥ 1, every admissible frame; no seminormality hypothesis).** With `i = i'`, at most one `κ_{i'}^{F_l}` is nonzero; `Σ_l κ_{i'}^{F_l}φ_{F_l} = 0` with pairwise independent `φ`'s then forces all three to vanish; so `Γ^{J,i'}` is constant across every triangle, hence on the connected sheet graph, hence `g_{i'} ∈ L ⊕ Ke_1` — contradiction. **The cheapest falsifier of (I) is dead; (I) survives its first test.**
> **Theorem 3 ((L2)_q on triangles, ∀k).** The three `φ_{F_l}` span a 2-dimensional `Φ_T = L ∩ W^⊥`; the `k` cocycle conditions collapse to one, `Σ_l ρ^lφ_{F_l} ∧ ξ_{F_l} = 0`; the solution space has dimension `3k − (2k−1) = k+1` = the dimension of the global solutions `w ↦ (w mod φ_{F_l})`. **Every solution on a triangle is global: a unique `w_T`.**
> **Theorem 4 (4-cycles).** On `Q6`, `Q8` the four `φ`'s span a 2-dimensional `Φ_Q`; the occurring `κ`-vectors span the full 2-dimensional relation space (the gap vectors `n_F ∈ N` of the two flats at a sheet are independent); the two resulting conditions force a unique `w_Q` **unless the cycle is doubly degenerate** (`φ_{F_2} ∝ φ_{F_0}` and `φ_{F_3} ∝ φ_{F_1}`). **(ND)**: no doubly-degenerate `Q6`. Generic admissible frames satisfy (ND).
> **Theorem 5 ((L2)_q under (ND), ∀k).** Triangles give `w_T`; `Q6`'s through a sheet with both sign patterns tie `w_{T(pp')} = w_{T(pp'')}` for pairs sharing a slot; connectivity gives one global `w`. **(L2)_q holds for every admissible frame with (ND).**
> **Theorem 6 (the local shortcut, ∀k, no (ND)).** `(L1)_q ⟹ (I)_q`: if every invisible element of degree `q` is `Δ_J`-divisible on each sheet, the per-sheet vectors `ξ'^J = B_J^{-T}x'^J` agree on triangles by Theorem 3, hence globally, hence `x − Σ_j ℓ_j^q w_j ∈ c_`. **Consequently (I) at `c = q` is PROBADO for `k ≤ 2`** (BOX_INVISIBILITY_REDUCTION Thm 4), and for `k ≥ 3` it is exactly (L1)_q.

> **Theorem 7 (the star lemma (L1)_q, ∀k ≥ 1, every odd `q`, every field of char ≠ 2; v2).** In the parity class `|ε| = 1` with odd slot `p_0`, let `F ∈ K[s]_{(q−1)/2}` be invisible on all diagonals, `β := [s_{p_0}^{(q−1)/2}]F`, `F̃ := F − βs_{p_0}^{(q−1)/2}`. Then `F̃` vanishes honestly on every non-star diagonal, and on each ray `F̃ = α̃_b s_{p_0}^{n'} + (s_{p_0} − s_b)F_b`; restricting to a non-star diagonal `D_{bb'}` and then to the corner `s_{p_0} = s_b` gives `α̃_b s_b^{n'} = 0`, so `α̃_b = 0`. Hence `F̃` vanishes honestly on all diagonals, `V | F̃`, `F ∈ (V) + 𝔟_ε`. All other parity classes at `c = q` are honest and Vandermonde-divisible directly. **(L1) holds at `c = q` for every `k`.**
> **Corollary 8 (THE WALL AT `c = q`, ∀k).** By Theorem 6: **`ann_Λ(g^{[q]})_q = c̄_q`** for every `k ≥ 1`, every admissible frame, every `q = p^v > k²`. With `H_1(ℓ^{[q]};Q)_q = 0` (GLUED_PURITY Thm 2): **`α_q = (2k+1)!!·C(q − k², k)`** — the annihilator law is exact at `c = q`, i.e. the Gorenstein mirror gives `A_{σ−q} = (2k+1)!!·C(q−k²,k)` at the TOP degree of the collar band, in every dimension.

Grades: Thms 1-3, 6, 7, Cor 8 PROBADO ∀k; Thm 4-5 PROBADO ∀k under (ND) (not needed for Cor 8). No number, no code, no measurement; no statement used the seminormality caveat (all conclusions are "no witnesses" or "in `c̄`").
