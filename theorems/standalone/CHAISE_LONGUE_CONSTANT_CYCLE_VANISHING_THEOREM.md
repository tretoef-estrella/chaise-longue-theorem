# CHAISE LONGUE — THE CONSTANT-CYCLE VANISHING THEOREM
### `(H_i)_{iq} = 0` for every `i ≥ 2`, and `(H_1)_q = K\cdot[p_q]` is a line — the Koszul homology of a Frobenius power over the odd-elementary CI never touches its trivial floor above degree one
### Constructor: **Bisel** · 25 August 2026 · Pending P0 · char `K ≠ 2`, `q` odd

**Certificado Ley 41.** Object: the graded piece `(H_i(x^{[q]};R))_{iq}` — the *constant* Koszul cycles of the Frobenius power over `R = S/E`. Cemetery grep by object: `DEFECT_TOR_LOCALIZATION` computes a `\mathrm{coker}` of `\operatorname{Tor}_1` and never touches graded floors; `CASCADE_COLLAPSE` is per sheet, where `(u_p^q)` **is** a regular sequence and all `H_i` vanish for `i≥1` — the opposite regime. No tomb shares the object. **Pillars:** (i) Odd Symmetric; (iii) via Recognition.

## 1. Setting
`\mathrm{char}\,K ≠ 2`, `q` odd, `n = 2k+2`, `E = (e_1,e_3,…,e_{2k+1})` radical CI, `R = S/E`, `A = R/(x^{[q]})R`.
`K_•` = Koszul complex of `x^{[q]} = (x_0^q,…,x_{n-1}^q)` over `R`, `K_i = R(-iq)^{\binom ni}`, `H_i = H_i(x^{[q]};R)`.
Since `K_i` is generated in degree `iq`, **`(H_i)_d = 0` for `d < iq`**, and `(H_i)_{iq}` is the space of cycles with **constant** coefficients. Call `iq` the *trivial floor*.

## 2. The degree-`q` line
> **LEMMA 1 (`∀k`, `∀q` odd).** `(H_1)_q = K\cdot[p_q]`, of dimension exactly `1`, where `p_q = \sum_s x_s^q`.

**Proof.** `(H_1)_q = \{λ ∈ K^n : \sum_s λ_s x_s^q ∈ E\}` (no Koszul boundaries exist below degree `2q`).
*Nonzero:* `p_m = 0` in `R` for every odd `m` (`SYMMETRIC_WRINKLE_LIFT`, all characteristics), and `q` is odd, so `λ = (1,…,1)` qualifies.
*Nothing else:* by **Recognition** (deposited, `∀k`), for coefficient degree `e = 0 < q` one has `\sum λ_s x_s^q ∈ E ⟺ λ_a - λ_b ∈ I(\{a,b\})` for every edge `\{a,b\}`. The `λ` are constants and `I(\{a,b\})` is generated by linear forms, so a constant lies in it only if it is `0`. Hence `λ_a = λ_b` for every edge; the edge graph is connected, so `λ` is constant. ∎

## 3. The vanishing theorem
> ### **THEOREM (`∀k`, `∀q` odd, char `≠ 2`).** `(H_i)_{iq} = 0` for every `i ≥ 2`.
> ### Equivalently: `\mathrm{bottom\text{-}deg}\,H_i > iq` for `i ≥ 2` — **the homology never reaches its trivial floor except at `i = 0,1`.**

**Proof.** Let `c = \sum_{|S|=i} c_S\,e_S` with `c_S ∈ K` be a cycle. For a subset `T` with `|T| = i-1`, the coefficient of `e_T` in `d(c)` is
`\displaystyle \sum_{s \notin T} \pm\,c_{T\cup s}\; x_s^{\,q} \;=\; 0 \ \text{ in } R_q.`
By Lemma 1 the space of constant relations among the `x_s^q` in `R_q` is exactly `K\cdot p_q`, so this combination equals `λ\,p_q = λ\sum_{\text{all } t} x_t^q` for some `λ ∈ K`. But the left-hand side contains **no** term `x_t^q` with `t ∈ T`, while `p_q` contains all of them. Since `i ≥ 2` we have `T ≠ ∅`, hence `λ = 0`, hence `\pm c_{T\cup s} = 0` for every `s \notin T`. Every `i`-subset is of the form `T\cup s`, so `c = 0`. ∎

**Remark.** The proof is characteristic-free beyond `≠ 2` (inherited from Recognition and from `E` radical) and uniform in `k`; it uses no gate and no measurement.

## 4. Why it matters — the staircase it starts
Pair this with the **Koszul–Poincaré duality** `H_{k+1-i} ≅ H_i^\vee(-a-nq)` (companion standalone, `a = k^2-1`) and with the fact that `H_1` is an `A`-module (`A` has top degree `T = (k+1)(q-1)`), so `\mathrm{top\text{-}deg}\,H_1 ≤ \mathrm{gen\text{-}deg}\,H_1 + T`. Then:
> **`\mathrm{gen\text{-}deg}\,H_1 ≤ q+2k` (the Degree Law) forces `\;\mathrm{bottom\text{-}deg}\,H_k \;≥\; (a+nq)-T-(q+2k) \;=\; k(k+q-1) \;=\; kq + k(k-1)`,**
an identity in `k` and `q` (checked at `(1,3),(1,5),(2,3),(3,3),(2,9)`).
> ### So the Degree Law needs the homology `H_k` to clear **exactly `k(k-1)` rungs above its trivial floor `kq`** — and this Theorem proves **the first rung, for every `k`**.
`k=1`: zero rungs, and `(H_1)_q ≠ 0` by Lemma 1 — **the Degree Law holds at `k=1` with equality, for free.**
`k=2`: two rungs (`(H_2)_{2q}`, `(H_2)_{2q+1}`); the first is proved here.
`k=3`: six rungs; the first is proved here.

## 5. Honest scope
Lemma 1 and the Theorem are **PROVED** `∀k ≥ 1`, `∀q` odd, char `≠ 2`, over any field, modulo the two deposited inputs cited (`SYMMETRIC_WRINKLE_LIFT`, `Recognition`). §4 is a **consequence chain**, not a proof of the Degree Law: the remaining `k(k-1)-1` rungs are open, and nothing here proves `A_k(q) = P_k(q)`.
